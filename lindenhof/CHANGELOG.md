### Added

- **AZ Lindenhof**, a complete website for a Flemish hospital, in Dutch: a home page that starts from what people come for, the care on offer and the care providers from A to Z, conditions and treatments explained for patients, the emergency department, your consultation, day admission and stay step by step, costs, rights and the ombudsman, visiting, campuses on a map that follows the site's colours, news, activities and jobs, with forms to ask for an appointment, sign up, apply, send a greeting card and get in touch. A search page with results by kind, activities that leave the list once they are over, and twelve sections to build new pages from. Built to WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark, with an accessibility statement to adapt.
