# Wilgendaal

A complete website for a Flemish city or municipality to make its own, written in Dutch: Stad Wilgendaal, a fictional town of 38,400 people in the valley of the river Mere. It follows how Flemish local governments work and write: services in the Flemish product structure, life events that gather what to arrange in the right order, an online application form, decision lists and public inquiries as notices, the mayor and aldermen, the Sociaal Huis, and an accessibility statement following the Bestuursdecreet. Its identity is its own: a W of two hanging willow branches on an orange tile for a logo, white and ink black with one warm brick orange and a deep blue for what you can do, large calm headings in Albert Sans, text in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (made for readers with low vision), soft corners, photographs given room, and panels framed by a narrow white margin, with a white tab cut into a photograph for its title or date. It is built for accessibility from the ground up: every page meets WCAG 2.2 at level AA in light and dark. Replace the city's name, words and pictures, and keep the rest.

## Install

```bash
composer require letterncms/wilgendaal
php cms site:apply wilgendaal --dry-run
php cms site:apply wilgendaal
```

Or choose **Wilgendaal** when you install (`php cms install --start-from=wilgendaal`), or under Design → Sites in the admin.

## What it brings

- **Pages**: a home page that opens on a photograph of willows on a square, with a white tab cut into it holding “Welkom in Wilgendaal”, the question “Wat wil je vandaag regelen?”, a search field and the quick actions, then the most asked services as tiles, the life events with their photographs, every theme on a black panel, news, events with their date in a tab on the photograph, and an orange panel for the open participation rounds; every other page opens with a grey panel holding the breadcrumbs, the title and what the page is about; Thema’s, with every service from A to Z by letter, searchable and filtered by theme; Levensmomenten; Nieuws; Activiteiten; Doe mee; Bestuur, with the city council and how decisions are made; Bekendmakingen; Contact, with every location on a map and whether it is open now; Iets melden; Afspraak maken; Online aanvragen; Zoeken; Over Wilgendaal; and the accessibility statement, privacy, cookies, complaints and a sitemap.
- **Collections**: Services (Diensten, 46, each with its cost, term, who it is for, how to apply, what to bring, an action button, where to go, an icon and the words people search for), Life events (6: moving, having a baby, living together or marrying, building, starting a business and a death, each with its steps and the services to arrange, in order), News (10, as news, press releases or road works), Events (8, listed until they are over, with date and time, venue, price, who it is for, booking and accessibility), Participation (3, open and closed, with the outcome), the board (the mayor, five aldermen and the chief executive), Locations (5 public buildings with opening hours, map position and accessibility) and Notices (10, each with a tagged PDF).
- **Taxonomies**: Thema’s, 10 categories with an icon, a photograph and a summary. Every theme has a page with its services, help and its news.
- **Templates**: Standaard, Pagina, Sectiepagina, Dienst, Thema, Levensmoment, Nieuwsbericht, Activiteit, Inspraak, Bestuurslid, Locatie, Bekendmaking and Niet gevonden.
- **Components**: everything that comes back is a component, changed in one place: the header, a white bar with soft corners that floats at the top of every page, with the logo, three quick links, search and a menu button whose lines turn into a cross, lying over the opening photograph or panel and casting a shadow once you scroll; the menu, which fills the screen with the main pages, every theme, search and the quick actions while only the bar stays above it; the footer, a black panel with the social media channels; the page head, the grey panel every page opens with, holding the breadcrumbs (a house, the pages above or the overview, and the page you are on), the title, what the page is about, a label and a date, and a photograph beside them where there is one; section heads; facts (as tiles or listed with an icon); opening hours that say whether a place is open now; pills; key figures; a help panel; the feedback question; and cards for services (numbered when they are steps), life events, news (a featured one, a card and a row), events, locations, people, participation and notices. Steps on a line are a component to insert and fill in.
- **Globals and menus**: the City, with its addresses, phone, email and opening hours; and the main, quick actions (Snel naar), footer, legal and social media menus.
- **Forms**: apply online, ask a question, report a problem with a photo, make an appointment, book a bulky waste collection, respond to a participation round, and page feedback.
- **Scripts**: `scrolled`, which tells the header the page is no longer at the top, and `open-now`, a small script that reads a location's own opening hours on the page and says whether it is open now, in Brussels time, on the contact page and every location.
- **The styling**: a Tailwind CSS theme in white and ink black with brick orange for panels, steps and icons and a deep blue for what you can do (links that lead on, the menu button, buttons and arrows under the pointer), colours for light and dark that keep every text at a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1, Albert Sans and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next at their normal spacing, one type scale and spacing rhythm, one row of soft corners from a label to a panel, framed panels (`slab`, `page-head`), the tab cut into a photograph with hollow corners (`notch`), cards that darken when pointed at (`card`) with a round arrow that turns blue (`go`), numbered steps (`steps`), facts as tiles (`facts`) or listed with their icons (`details`), links as pills (`pill`), a search field as one rounded box (`search-box`), photographs that grow a little when pointed at (`zoom`), Dutch hyphenation in headings, a clear focus line, buttons, form fields, and colour schemes for sections (`scheme-night`, `scheme-accent`, `scheme-soft`).
- **Media**: 31 photographs and 10 documents, with their Dutch alt text, and the city's icon.
