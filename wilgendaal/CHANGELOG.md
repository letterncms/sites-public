### Added

- **Wilgendaal, a Flemish city's website, in Dutch.** A complete site for a Flemish city or municipality to start from, built for accessibility and meeting WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark. A home page that welcomes you on a photograph of the town, with search, the quick actions and the most asked services; six life events (moving, a baby, living together or marrying, building, a business, a death) that put what to arrange with the city in order; 46 services under 10 themes in the Flemish product structure (cost, term, who for, how to apply, what to bring) and an A to Z by letter; an online application form; news with press releases and road works, upcoming events, participation with a response form, the mayor and aldermen and how the council decides, locations with live opening status, accessibility details and maps, notices (decision lists, regulations, public inquiries) with tagged PDFs, forms for questions, reports, appointments and bulky waste, a feedback question on every page, and an accessibility statement following the Flemish Bestuursdecreet and the EU model. Its own identity, with a W of two hanging willow branches for a logo: white and ink black with brick orange and a deep blue for what you can do, Albert Sans for headings and Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for text, soft corners, large photographs and black, orange and grey panels framed by a narrow white margin, a white tab cut into a photograph for its title or date, a header that floats as a white bar over every page, a menu that fills the screen, breadcrumbs that end with the page you are on, and links to the city's social media.
