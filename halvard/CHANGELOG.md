# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Added

- **Halvard**, a complete website for a hospital: specialties in six areas of care, a doctor finder, conditions and treatments explained for patients, the emergency department, practical pages for patients and visitors, news, events, vacancies and locations, with forms to ask for an appointment, sign up for an event, apply for a job and get in touch. Conditions and treatments from A to Z, events that leave the list once they are over, and the hospital's mark as the favicon. Built to WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark, with an accessibility statement to adapt.
