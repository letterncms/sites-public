You make MRI scans of patients of every age, for every department. You prepare patients for their scan, keep them comfortable and safe, and make sure the images are what the radiologist needs.

## You

- Are a registered radiographer, ideally with experience in MRI
- Explain things clearly and kindly
- Can work 24 to 32 hours a week, including some evenings

## What we offer

- A salary on the national scale for your role, with a year-end bonus
- 27 days of holiday a year, more with years of service
- A training budget and time to learn, in our own academy
- Help with childcare, and discounts on public transport and a bike
