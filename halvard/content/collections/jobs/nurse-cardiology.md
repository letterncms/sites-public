## You

- Are a registered nurse, or will be within six months
- Enjoy working in a close team
- Want to grow in heart care: we train you

## What we offer

- A salary on the national scale for your role, with a year-end bonus
- 27 days of holiday a year, more with years of service
- A training budget and time to learn, in our own academy
- Help with childcare, and discounts on public transport and a bike
