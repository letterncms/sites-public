Every day, hundreds of patients move through the hospital: to a scan, to the operating theatre, to their ward. You take them there, in a bed or a wheelchair, and often you are the person who puts them at ease on the way.

## You

- Are friendly and patient
- Are fit and like to be on the move
- Can work in shifts, including weekends

## What we offer

- A salary on the national scale for your role, with a year-end bonus
- 27 days of holiday a year, more with years of service
- A training budget and time to learn, in our own academy
- Help with childcare, and discounts on public transport and a bike
