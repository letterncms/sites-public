Our main campus has four buildings, joined by covered walkways. Every department has a route number: follow the signs from the main hall, or ask at reception, where volunteers can walk with you.

## In the main hall

- Reception and check-in kiosks
- A café, a shop and a pharmacy
- A quiet room for prayer and reflection
- Wheelchairs to borrow, free of charge
