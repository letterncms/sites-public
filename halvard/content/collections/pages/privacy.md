This statement is about this website. How we handle your medical information in the hospital is explained in the patient privacy leaflet, which you can ask for at reception.

## What we collect

- **When you send a form**, the details you fill in, the page you sent it from, and the language and type of your browser. We never store your IP address.
- **When you visit a page**, nothing that identifies you, unless you allow statistics cookies.

## Why

We use what you send to answer you: to arrange an appointment, to book your place at an event, to read your application or to reply to your message. We keep form submissions for as long as we need them, and at most two years.

## Cookies

This website uses only the cookies it needs to work, unless you agree to more. When you first visit, you choose whether we may count visits anonymously. You can change your choice at any time.

## Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal details we hold about you, and object to how we use them. Contact our data protection officer at privacy@halvard.example. You can also complain to the data protection authority.
