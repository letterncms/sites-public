Halvard Hospital wants everyone to be able to use this website, whatever their ability or the device they use. This statement applies to this website and to the forms on it.

## How accessible this website is

This website is **partially compliant** with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, which include everything the European standard EN 301 549, version 3.2.1, asks of a website. What is not yet compliant is listed below.

You should be able to:

- use the whole website with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus
- use it with a screen reader, such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack
- zoom in up to 400% without text overflowing or content being cut off
- read all text with enough contrast, in light and in dark mode
- skip straight to the main content with the first link on every page
- fill in every form without a time limit, with clear labels and errors that say how to fix them
- use the website without animations, if your device is set to reduce motion

## What is not yet accessible

- **Directions.** The directions link on our location pages opens OpenStreetMap, a service of others that we can’t make fully accessible. Every location page gives the address, the bus routes and parking in text.
- **Older documents.** Some leaflets in PDF published before 23 September 2018 are not fully accessible. They are exempt by law, but if you need one in another format, ask us and we send it within five working days.

## Accessibility in the hospital

This website is one part of it. In the hospital, you can borrow a wheelchair at every entrance, use hearing loops at reception desks and in the auditorium, and bring your assistance dog everywhere except the operating theatres and intensive care. Ask for a sign language or spoken language interpreter when you make an appointment: it is free.

## Tell us about a barrier

If something on this website doesn’t work for you, or you need information in another format, such as large print, easy read, audio or braille, please tell us. We reply within five working days.

- Use the <a>contact form</a> and choose “The accessibility of this website”
- Email accessibility@halvard.example
- Call +44 20 7946 0100, Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00

## Enforcement

If you are not satisfied with our reply, you can contact the national body that oversees the accessibility of public sector websites and services. It handles complaints free of charge.

## How we prepared this statement

This statement was prepared on 16 November 2026. It is based on a self-assessment: automated testing of every page against WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark mode, and manual testing with a keyboard, with NVDA and VoiceOver, at 400% zoom and on small screens. We review this statement every year, and whenever we change the website a great deal. The next review is planned for November 2027.
