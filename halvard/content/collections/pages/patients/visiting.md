## Please help us keep patients safe

- Come with no more than two people at a time.
- Clean your hands when you arrive and when you leave.
- Stay at home if you have a fever, a cough or diarrhoea.
- Flowers are welcome, except in intensive care and on the cancer ward.

::: callout {"props":{"text":"During flu season, please don’t visit if you feel unwell, even a little. A video call is a lovely way to stay close.","tone":"warning"}}
:::
