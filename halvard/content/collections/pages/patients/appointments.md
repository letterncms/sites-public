## Making an appointment

For most specialties, you need a referral letter from your GP for a first appointment. With your letter, you can call the appointments line, or ask for an appointment below and we call you back within two working days.

For maternity care, the emergency department, blood tests and X-rays, you don’t need an appointment or a referral.

::: callout {"props":{"text":"Can’t come? Please tell us at least 24 hours before, so someone else can have your time.","tone":"tip"}}
:::

## On the day

- Come fifteen minutes early.
- Check in at a kiosk in the main hall with your ID, or at reception.
- Follow the route number on your letter to the waiting area. Volunteers in red jackets can walk with you.
- Bring your ID, your insurance card, your referral letter and a list of your medicines.
