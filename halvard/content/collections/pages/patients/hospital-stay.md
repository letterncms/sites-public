## Before you come in

Your ward calls you a few days before to confirm the date and time. If you need to stop any medicines or not eat before an operation, your letter tells you.

## What to pack

- Comfortable clothes, slippers and nightwear
- Toiletries, glasses and hearing aids, with their cases
- Your medicines, in their boxes
- A phone and its charger, and something to read
- Leave jewellery and large sums of money at home

## Your room

About a third of our rooms are single rooms, each with its own shower. Every bed has a screen with free television, radio and wifi. Wifi is free everywhere in the hospital: choose the network called Halvard Guest.

## Meals

You choose your meals each day from a menu on your bedside screen, with vegetarian, halal and kosher dishes, and meals for every diet your doctor prescribes.

## Going home

Most people go home before 11:00. Before you leave, you get a letter for your GP, your medicines and a phone number to call with questions.
