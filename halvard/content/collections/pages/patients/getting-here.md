## By public transport

Buses 12, 34 and 56 stop at the main entrance. From Elmsworth Central station, it is a ten-minute walk or one stop on bus 12.

## By bike

There are covered bike racks at the main entrance and at the emergency department, with chargers for electric bikes.

## By car

Follow the signs for Halvard Hospital from the ring road. Car park P1 is by the main entrance; P2 is by the emergency department.

Blue Badge holders park free in the 40 accessible spaces near the entrances. Parents of children on the children’s ward and families of patients in intensive care can ask for a free pass at reception.
