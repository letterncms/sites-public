## Your rights

You have the right to clear information about your health and your treatment, to decide about your care, to a second opinion, and to privacy. You can always bring someone you trust to an appointment.

## Your medical record

You can read your own record and ask for a copy, free of charge. Ask through the contact form, choosing “My medical records”, and we reply within a month.

## Praise and complaints

We like to hear what went well, and what didn’t. Tell the people who cared for you first: many things can be put right straight away. If that doesn’t help, our patient liaison team listens and looks for a solution with you.

Use the <a>contact form</a> and choose “Praise or thanks” or “A complaint”. You hear from us within five working days.

## Independent advice

If you are not satisfied with how we handled your complaint, you can turn to the independent health ombudsman, free of charge.
