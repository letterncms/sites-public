## What is covered

Most care in our hospital is covered by your health insurance. Your GP’s referral letter and your insurance card are all we need. Some treatments, such as cosmetic procedures, are not covered: your doctor always tells you before.

## Your bill

We send the bill for your care straight to your insurer. You only get a bill yourself when part of your care is not covered, or when you have an excess. You can pay it by bank transfer, online or at the payments desk in the main hall.
