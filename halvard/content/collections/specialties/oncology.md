Hearing that you have cancer changes everything. From the first day, you have one specialist nurse who knows your story and answers your questions, by phone or in person. Your doctors from every specialty meet each week to agree the best treatment for you, and you decide on it together with them.

## Treatments

- Chemotherapy and immunotherapy in our day hospital
- Surgery, with our surgical teams
- Radiotherapy, with our partner centre in Elmsworth
- Hormone therapy and targeted medicines

## Support

Living with cancer is about more than treatment. Our psychologists, dietitians, social workers and physiotherapists are part of the team, and our walk-in centre on the ground floor offers a cup of tea, a wig consultant and a place to talk with people who have been through it.
