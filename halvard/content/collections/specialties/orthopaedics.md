Our orthopaedic surgeons treat problems of the bones and joints, at every age. We always look at what you can do without an operation first, with exercise, physiotherapy or an injection. When you do need surgery, such as a new hip or knee, most people walk on the day of their operation and go home within two days.

## What we treat

- Worn hips and knees (osteoarthritis)
- Sports injuries of the knee, shoulder and ankle
- Broken bones, together with the emergency department
- Back and neck problems
- Hand and wrist problems, such as carpal tunnel syndrome

## Joint replacement school

Before a new hip or knee, you and someone close to you join a morning at our joint school. A surgeon, a physiotherapist and a nurse explain the operation and your recovery, and you practise with crutches. People who come recover faster and worry less.
