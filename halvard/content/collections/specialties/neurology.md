Our neurologists treat people with conditions of the brain and nerves, from a first seizure to long-term care for multiple sclerosis. Our stroke unit is open day and night: when someone arrives with a stroke, a neurologist, a scan and the right treatment are ready within minutes.

## What we treat

- Stroke and mini-strokes (TIA)
- Epilepsy and first seizures
- Migraine and other headaches
- Parkinson’s disease and movement disorders
- Multiple sclerosis
- Numbness, weakness and nerve pain

## Tests

Many people have a scan of the brain (MRI or CT), a recording of the brain’s activity (EEG) or a test of the nerves and muscles (EMG). We try to do these on the same day as your appointment.
