After a stroke, an operation, an accident or a long illness, our rehabilitation team helps you get back to the things that matter to you. You set your goals together with us: walking to the shops, going back to work, playing with your grandchildren.

## Our team

- Physiotherapists, for strength, balance and moving
- Occupational therapists, for everyday tasks at home and at work
- Speech and language therapists, for speaking and swallowing
- Psychologists and social workers, for everything that comes with it

## Where we see you

On the wards, at the outpatient clinic in the main building, and at our <a>Rehabilitation Centre in Linden Park</a>, which has a gym, a hydrotherapy pool and a practice flat with a kitchen.
