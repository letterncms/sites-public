As we grow older, health problems often come together: a fall, several medicines, a memory that slips. Our geriatricians look at all of it at once, together with you and the people close to you, and agree a plan that fits your life.

## What we look at

- Falls and fear of falling
- Memory problems and confusion
- Many medicines at once
- Weight loss and losing strength
- How you manage at home

## The geriatric day clinic

In one day, you see a geriatrician, a nurse, a physiotherapist and, when needed, an occupational therapist and a dietitian. At the end of the day we talk through what we found and what will help. Bring someone who knows you well.
