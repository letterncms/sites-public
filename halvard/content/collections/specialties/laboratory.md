Our laboratory analyses around 4,000 samples a day: blood, urine and tissue, for every department of the hospital and for GPs in the valley. Most results are ready for your doctor within a few hours.

## Having a blood test

Walk in with your form during opening hours, or book a time to avoid waiting. Take a ticket at the entrance of the blood test room on route 030. If your doctor asked you to fast, don’t eat for eight hours before; you can drink water.
