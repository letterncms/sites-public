Our intensive care unit has twenty-four beds for people who need machines to help them breathe or keep their heart and kidneys working, after a major operation, an accident or a severe illness. Doctors are on the unit day and night, and every patient has a nurse close by all the time.

## For families

Seeing someone you love in intensive care is hard. You can visit at any time, after calling the unit. Every day, a doctor talks with you about how your relative is doing; we also keep a diary for each patient, which many find helps them later to understand what happened.
