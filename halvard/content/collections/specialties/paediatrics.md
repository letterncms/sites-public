Our paediatricians, children’s nurses and play specialists care for children with short illnesses and with conditions that need care for years, such as asthma, diabetes and allergies. Every room on the children’s ward has a bed for a parent, and there is a playroom, a school teacher and a quiet room for teenagers.

## What we treat

- Asthma, allergies and eczema
- Childhood diabetes
- Problems with growing and eating
- Infections that need a stay in hospital
- Babies born early or small, in our neonatal unit

## Preparing your child

Tell your child honestly what will happen, in words they understand. A favourite toy or blanket helps. Our play specialists can prepare your child for a blood test or an operation with pictures and play; ask for them when you book.
