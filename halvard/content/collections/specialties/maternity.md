More than two thousand babies are born at Halvard every year. Most births are led by midwives in our birth centre, with a birthing pool and a double bed in every room, so your partner can stay. When you or your baby need more care, an obstetrician and a neonatal team are on the same floor, day and night.

## During your pregnancy

You can register with us yourself from the moment you know you are pregnant; you don’t need a referral. Your midwife sees you about ten times during your pregnancy and arranges your scans at twelve and twenty weeks.

## Giving birth

Come to the birth centre when your contractions are regular and about five minutes apart, when your waters break, or whenever you are worried. Call us first, so we are ready for you. Most mothers and babies go home within a day; after a caesarean, most stay two or three days.

## Classes for parents-to-be

Our midwives run classes on giving birth, feeding your baby and the first weeks at home, and tours of the birth centre. See <a>events and classes</a> for the next dates.
