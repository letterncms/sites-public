Our gynaecologists see women with problems of the womb, ovaries and pelvic floor, and work closely with our fertility and cancer teams. Many conditions can be treated with medicines or a short procedure, and when you need an operation we use keyhole surgery wherever we can, so you recover faster.

## What we treat

- Heavy, painful or irregular periods
- Endometriosis and fibroids
- Problems getting pregnant
- Complaints during and after the menopause
- Prolapse and urine leakage
- Abnormal smear test results

## Your first appointment

You talk with the gynaecologist about your symptoms first. An examination and an ultrasound scan may follow, in the same visit; you can always ask for a female doctor or for someone to be with you.
