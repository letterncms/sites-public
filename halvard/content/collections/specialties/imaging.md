Our radiographers and radiologists make and read more than 200,000 images a year. Many tests take a few minutes. Your own doctor discusses the results with you, usually within a week.

## Tests we do

- X-rays: walk in with your form, no appointment needed
- Ultrasound scans
- CT scans
- MRI scans, now on our quieter new scanner
- Mammography and breast ultrasound
- Treatments guided by imaging, such as some biopsies

## Before your scan

Your appointment letter says if you need to fast or drink water before the test. Tell us before an MRI if you have a pacemaker, a metal implant or might be pregnant. You can bring your own music for an MRI.
