Our cardiologists, heart surgeons and specialist nurses look after people with every kind of heart condition, from a racing heartbeat that comes and goes to heart failure that needs care for years. Most people see us at the outpatient clinic and go home the same day; when you need a procedure or an operation, the same team stays with you.

## What we treat

- Chest pain and angina
- Heart rhythm problems, such as atrial fibrillation
- Heart failure
- Valve disease
- High blood pressure that is hard to control
- Heart problems you were born with, in adults

## Your first appointment

Your first visit takes about an hour and a half. We usually record a heart trace (ECG) and may make an ultrasound of your heart (an echocardiogram) on the same morning, so that the cardiologist can discuss the results with you straight away.

## Heart failure nurses

People with heart failure have a nurse of their own, who checks in by phone and teaches you how to watch your weight, your breathing and your medicines at home. Since this year, some patients also measure their blood pressure and weight at home with a monitor that sends the readings to us.
