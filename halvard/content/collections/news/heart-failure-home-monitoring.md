Since spring, people with heart failure can join a trial in which they measure their weight and blood pressure at home each morning. A small device sends the readings to our heart failure nurses, who call when something changes.

Early results are encouraging: people in the trial have needed fewer emergency admissions than before. The trial runs until the end of next year, together with three other hospitals in the region.

“Patients tell us they feel safer, because they know someone is keeping an eye on them,” says cardiologist Dr Arjun Mehta.
