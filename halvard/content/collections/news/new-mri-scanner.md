Since this month, our imaging department has a new MRI scanner. It replaces a scanner that served the hospital for fourteen years.

The new scanner has a wider opening, which many people who feel anxious in small spaces find easier. It is also much quieter, and most scans now take half as long, so we can see around thirty more patients each week.

Patients helped choose it. “We asked people who had a scan on the old machine what mattered to them,” says radiologist Dr Lucas Fontaine. “Noise came first, then space.”
