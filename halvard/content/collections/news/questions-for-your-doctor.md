Many people remember only part of what their doctor told them. Writing down your questions beforehand helps, and so does bringing someone with you. These five questions are a good start.

1. **What is wrong with me?** Ask your doctor to explain it in plain words.
2. **What are my options?** Including doing nothing for now.
3. **What are the benefits and risks of each option?**
4. **How likely are those to happen to me?**
5. **What happens next, and who do I call if I have questions?**

Your doctor would rather answer one question too many than have you go home worried. You can also ask for the most important things in writing.
