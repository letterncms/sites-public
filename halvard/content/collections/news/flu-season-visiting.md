Visits from family and friends help patients get better. During flu season, a visitor with a cold can also make someone very ill, so we ask everyone to take a few simple steps.

- Stay at home if you have a fever, a cough or a sore throat.
- Clean your hands when you come in and when you leave.
- Come with no more than two people at a time.
- Children under twelve are welcome, but only when they are well.

On some wards, such as intensive care and the cancer ward, other rules may apply. Ask the nurses when you arrive.
