## What happens

You lie on a table that moves through a ring. The scan itself takes a few minutes. Sometimes you get a contrast fluid through a drip in your arm, which can give you a warm feeling for a moment.

## Before your scan

Your letter says whether you need to fast. Tell us if you might be pregnant, have kidney problems or have reacted to contrast fluid before.
