## What it is

Some women develop high blood sugar while they are pregnant. You usually don’t feel it, which is why we test for it around 26 weeks when you have a higher chance of it.

## How we treat it

A dietitian helps you adjust what you eat, and you learn to check your blood sugar at home. Some women also need tablets or insulin. Your midwife and obstetrician keep an extra eye on your baby’s growth.
