## What it is

The ligaments hold your knee together. A twist during football, skiing or a fall can tear one, most often the front cruciate ligament. The knee swells quickly and may feel as if it gives way.

## How we treat it

Many people do well with physiotherapy alone. Young, active people and those whose knee keeps giving way may need an operation to rebuild the ligament, followed by six to nine months of rehabilitation.
