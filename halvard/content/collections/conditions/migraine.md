## What it is

Migraine is more than a headache: attacks of throbbing pain, often on one side, with nausea and a strong dislike of light and sound, lasting hours to days. Some people see flashing lights or zigzags first (an aura).

## How we treat it

We look at what triggers your attacks and at medicines to stop an attack and to prevent new ones, including the newer injections for people with frequent migraine.
