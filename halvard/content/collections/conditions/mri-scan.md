## What happens

You lie on a table that slides into a wide tube. The scanner makes loud knocking sounds; you get headphones and can listen to music. A scan takes 20 to 45 minutes and doesn’t hurt.

## Before your scan

Tell us if you have a pacemaker, metal implants or fragments, or might be pregnant. Leave jewellery and cards with a magnetic strip at home or in a locker. If you feel anxious in small spaces, tell us when you book: our new scanner is wider, and we can help.
