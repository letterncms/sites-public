## The procedure

The cardiologist guides thin tubes (catheters) through a vein in your groin to your heart, finds the spots that cause the irregular rhythm and treats them with heat or cold. It takes two to four hours, under sedation or general anaesthetic.

## Afterwards

Most people go home the next day and return to normal activities within a week. It can take three months to know how well it worked.
