## The operation

The surgeon replaces the worn ball and socket of your hip with an artificial joint. The operation takes about an hour, usually with a spinal anaesthetic so you are awake but feel nothing, or asleep if you prefer.

## Recovery

Most people stand and walk with a physiotherapist on the day of the operation and go home after one or two days. You walk with crutches for a few weeks. After about six weeks, most people drive and do their daily tasks again.
