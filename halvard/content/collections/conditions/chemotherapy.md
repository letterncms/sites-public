## How it works

Chemotherapy medicines travel through your body and attack cells that divide quickly, such as cancer cells. You usually have them in cycles, with rest weeks in between, through a drip or as tablets.

## In the day hospital

Most treatments take a few hours, in a comfortable chair, with someone with you if you like. Your nurse explains the side effects to watch for and how to reach our advice line, day and night.
