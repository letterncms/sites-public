## Signs

Coughing, especially at night, wheezing, a tight chest and getting out of breath quickly when playing.

## How we treat it

Most children do well with an inhaler that prevents attacks, used every day, and a second one for when symptoms come. At our asthma clinic, children and parents learn how and when to use them, and what to do during an attack.
