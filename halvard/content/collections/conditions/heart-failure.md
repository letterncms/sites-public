## What it is

Heart failure doesn’t mean your heart has stopped: it pumps less strongly than it should. Fluid can build up in your lungs and legs, so you get breathless, tired and swollen ankles.

## How we treat it

Medicines help your heart work better and remove extra fluid. Your heart failure nurse teaches you to weigh yourself every morning and to spot early signs that you need help. Some people get a pacemaker that helps both sides of the heart beat together.
