## What it is

In epilepsy, the brain’s electrical activity is briefly disturbed, causing a seizure. A seizure can be a short absence, a twitch of one arm, or a fit with loss of consciousness.

## After a first seizure

Our first seizure clinic sees you within two weeks, with an EEG and often an MRI scan. Not everyone who has one seizure has epilepsy.

## How we treat it

Most people become seizure-free with medicine. For those who don’t, we look at other options together with a specialist epilepsy centre.
