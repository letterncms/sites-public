## What it is

A stroke happens when a blood vessel in the brain is blocked by a clot or bursts. Without blood, brain cells die within minutes, which is why fast treatment matters so much.

## How we treat it

When an ambulance brings someone with a stroke, our stroke team is waiting. A scan shows what kind of stroke it is. A clot can often be dissolved with medicine or removed through a thin tube, within the first hours. Afterwards, our stroke unit and rehabilitation team help with recovery.
