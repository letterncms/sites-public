## Why people fall

Falls often have several causes at once: weaker muscles, poorer balance, medicines that make you dizzy, low blood pressure when you stand up, poor eyesight or a loose rug.

## How we help

At our falls clinic, a geriatrician, a physiotherapist and an occupational therapist look for the causes and work on them with you, from balance training to fewer medicines and changes at home.
