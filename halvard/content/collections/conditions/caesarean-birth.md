## Planned or unplanned

Some caesareans are planned, for example when the baby lies bottom-first. Others happen during labour, when it is the safest way for your baby to be born.

## On the day

Most women are awake, with a spinal anaesthetic, and your partner can be with you. Your baby is placed on your chest as soon as possible. You usually stay two or three days, and recovery at home takes about six weeks.
