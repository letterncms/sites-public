## What it is

In atrial fibrillation, the upper chambers of the heart beat irregularly and often too fast. Some people feel their heart racing or fluttering, or get tired and breathless; others notice nothing at all.

## Why it matters

Atrial fibrillation can let small clots form in the heart, which raises the risk of a stroke. Medicines that thin the blood lower that risk a great deal.

## How we treat it

- Medicines to thin the blood and to slow or steady the heartbeat
- A cardioversion: a short, controlled shock under sedation that resets the rhythm
- A catheter ablation, which can stop it for good in many people
