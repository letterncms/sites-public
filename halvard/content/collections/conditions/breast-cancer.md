## Signs

Check your breasts regularly. See your GP if you find a lump, a change in the shape or skin of a breast, a nipple that turns inwards, or fluid from a nipple.

## At the breast clinic

At our one-stop breast clinic, you see a breast surgeon, have a mammogram and an ultrasound, and if needed a biopsy, in one morning. You hear most results the same day.

## Treatment

Depending on the type of cancer: surgery, radiotherapy, chemotherapy, hormone therapy or targeted medicines, often in combination. Your specialist breast nurse is with you throughout.
