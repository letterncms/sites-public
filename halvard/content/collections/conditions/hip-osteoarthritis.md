## What it is

In osteoarthritis, the smooth layer of cartilage in the joint slowly wears away. You get pain in your groin or thigh when you walk, and stiffness in the morning.

## How we treat it

We start with what you can do yourself: exercise, losing weight if needed, and painkillers. Physiotherapy keeps the muscles around the hip strong. When pain keeps you from living the way you want, a new hip is a very successful operation.
