## What happens

A phlebotomist takes a little blood from a vein in your arm. It takes a few minutes. Walk in with your form during opening hours, or book a time.

## Before the test

Your doctor tells you if you need to fast. If so, don’t eat for eight hours before, and drink only water. Take your medicines as usual unless you were told otherwise.
