### Added

- **Lindevoorde, a Flemish city's website, in Dutch.** A complete site for a Flemish city or municipality to start from, built for accessibility and meeting WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark: a search-first home page (“Waarmee kunnen we je helpen?”) with the top tasks, 36 products under 10 themes in the Flemish product structure (cost, term, who for, how to apply, what to bring) and an A to Z by letter, real search with a results page, news with press releases and road works, upcoming events, participation with a response form, the mayor and aldermen and how the council decides, locations with opening hours, accessibility details and maps, notices (decision lists, regulations, public inquiries) with tagged PDFs, an e-loket overview, forms for questions, reports, appointments and bulky waste, a feedback question on every page, an alert bar, and an accessibility statement following the Flemish Bestuursdecreet and the EU model. Set in Instrument Sans, in city blue with a brick-red accent and the stepped gable of Flemish brick houses as its motif, with photographs throughout.
