Don’t throw it away: bring it to the repair café. Volunteers with years of experience help you fix it, and show you how to do it yourself next time.

## What you can bring

- small electrical items, such as lamps, toasters and radios
- clothes and bags that need a stitch or a new zip
- bikes with flat tyres, loose brakes or a slipping chain
- toys, jewellery and wooden furniture small enough to carry

Bring any parts you already have. We can’t repair gas appliances or anything that needs a big spare part.

## How it works

Sign in at the desk when you arrive. Bring one item at first, so everyone gets a turn. While you wait, have a coffee and meet your neighbours. The last item is taken at 12:30.

The repair café is on the third Saturday of every month. Repairs are free. A small donation helps pay for tools and parts.
