During the school holiday week, the children’s floor of the library turns into a story theatre. Each session lasts 45 minutes and has a different storyteller.

## When

- Monday 15 to Friday 19 February: 10:30 and 14:00
- Saturday 20 February: 10:30

## What to expect

There are tales from around the world, songs to join in with, and puppets made by children from last year’s workshop. Some stories are told in 2 languages. After each session, children can borrow the books the stories came from.

## Booking

Sessions are free, but places are limited to 30 children. Book a place with the [contact form](/contact) and tell us which day and time you would like. An adult needs to stay with children under 6.

If you can’t come after all, let us know so someone else can have your place.
