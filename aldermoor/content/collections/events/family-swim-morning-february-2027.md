Spend a Sunday morning in the water at the newly renovated Riverside Pool. Everyone pays €3, adults and children alike, and children under 4 go free.

## What’s on

- a giant inflatable obstacle course in the main pool
- games, floats and water toys in the learner pool, with the floor set shallow for little ones
- free 15-minute taster lessons with our swimming teachers, for children aged 5 to 12

You don’t need to book. Just pay at the door.

## Good to know

Children under 8 must have an adult with them in the water, and one adult can bring up to 2 children under 8. Lifeguards are on duty all morning.

The lockers are free and use a code you choose. You can buy drinks and snacks in the café on the viewing balcony.
