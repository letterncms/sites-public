The winter market fills the north side of Market Square from 28 November to 3 January. Mayor Helen Achterberg switches on the tree lights at 17:00 on the opening day.

## Opening times

The market is open every day from 11:00 to 21:00. On 24 and 31 December it closes at 17:00, and it is closed on 25 December and 1 January.

## What’s on

- about 60 stalls run by local makers and bakers
- a small skating rink for children up to 12, free, with skates to borrow
- live music on Friday evenings from 18:00, and at weekends

Drinks come in cups you can use again: pay €2 extra and get it back when you return the cup.

The weekly market carries on as usual on Wednesday and Saturday, on the south side of the square. Visit our stand to see the designs for a greener Market Square.

## Getting there

There is no car parking on the square. Trams 2 and 4 stop at Market Square.
