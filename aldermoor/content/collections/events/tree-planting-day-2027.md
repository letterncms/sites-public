Help us finish this winter’s planting with a day outdoors in Moorside Park. Together we will plant 40 young trees and 300 metres of hedge along the east side of the park.

## The day

Meet at the main gate on Park Avenue at 10:00. Our tree team shows you how to plant a tree so it grows well. Then we work in small groups. Free soup and bread are served at 12:30.

You can come for an hour or stay all day.

## What to bring

Wear old clothes and sturdy boots or wellies. We provide spades, gloves and water. Children are very welcome, with an adult. We have small tools for them too.

## Sign up

Use the [contact form](/contact), so we know how many tools and how much soup to bring. If it rains hard, we move to Saturday 20 March and tell everyone who signed up.
