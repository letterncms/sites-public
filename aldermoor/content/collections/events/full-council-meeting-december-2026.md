The city council meets in public for the last time this year. Anyone can come and listen. You don’t need to book: the public gallery has 80 seats, and doors open at 19:00.

## On the agenda

- the 3 designs for a greener Market Square, and what residents have said about them so far
- winter support for residents, including help with energy bills and extra night shelter places
- questions from residents

The full agenda and papers are in [documents](/documents) from Monday 7 December.

## Ask a question or speak

To ask a question or speak about an item, send it with the [contact form](/contact) by 12:00 on Monday 14 December. Find out how on [ask a question at a council meeting](/services/ask-a-question-at-a-council-meeting).

## Watch from home

The meeting is streamed live on the [council](/council) page, and you can watch it again afterwards.
