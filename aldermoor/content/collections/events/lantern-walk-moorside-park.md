Light up the darkest time of the year with a walk through Moorside Park. We meet at the main gate on Park Avenue at 16:30, just as it gets dark.

## Make your own lantern

Before the walk, come to the lantern workshop at the Central Library, Library Lane 2, from 13:00 to 15:30. It’s free and we provide everything you need. No need to book.

If you don’t have a lantern, we have 200 to give away at the gate, while they last.

## The walk

The walk is 1.5 km and takes about 45 minutes, with music along the way. Young children and anyone who wants a shorter walk can take the 600-metre route, which is marked with blue lights.

At the end, by the pond, there is free hot chocolate and live music from the youth centre bands.

## Good to know

Dress warmly. Candles aren’t allowed, so bring a battery light instead. If the weather is very bad, we post any change on [news](/news) by 12:00 on the day.
