Market Square is the heart of Aldermoor, but in summer it is one of the hottest places in the city, and it is hard to cross with a pushchair or a wheelchair. We have 3 designs for a new square, and nothing has been decided yet.

## The 3 designs

- **Shade and seating**: 24 large trees, benches all around and a water play area
- **Market first**: room for 80 market stalls, with trees along the edges and a covered area for events
- **A green link**: a park from City Hall to the river, with fewer stalls

## The evening

- 19:00: the design team explains the 3 designs
- 19:30: talk with the designers and council staff at tables
- 20:30: what we heard, and what happens next

Children are welcome, with a play corner. Book a place with the [contact form](/contact), or just come along.
