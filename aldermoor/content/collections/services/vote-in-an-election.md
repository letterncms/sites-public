## Who can vote

You can vote in city council elections if, on election day:

- you are 18 or older
- you live in Aldermoor
- you are a citizen of this country, or you are from another country and have lived here legally for at least 5 years

You don’t need to register to vote. When you register your address with us, you are added to the electoral register. Different rules apply to national elections.

City council elections are every 4 years. The next one is on Thursday 16 March 2028.

## Your polling card

Your polling card arrives by post about 2 weeks before election day. It shows the polling station nearest to your home. You can vote at any polling station in Aldermoor.

If your card hasn’t arrived 5 days before the election, or you lost it, call 01632 960000 for a new one.

## On election day

Polling stations are open from 7:30 to 21:00. Bring your polling card and your ID card, passport or driving licence. Your ID can be up to 5 years out of date.

Every polling station is step-free. Ask the staff for a magnifier, a large-print ballot paper or a seat. If you can’t mark your ballot because of a disability, you can bring someone to help you in the booth.

## Vote by post

You can vote by post if you will be away or can’t get to a polling station. Apply with the [contact form](/contact) or by letter at least 6 weeks before election day. Your ballot paper arrives about 3 weeks before. Post it back so it reaches us by election day, or hand it in at any polling station.

## Let someone vote for you

You can let another voter in Aldermoor vote for you. This is called a proxy vote. Sign the back of your polling card and give it to them, with a copy of your ID. They vote at the same time as they cast their own vote. Each person can vote for up to 2 other people.
