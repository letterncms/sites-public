Your parking notice shows the fine number, the amount, and where and when we saw your car.

## Pay a fine

| When you pay | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Within 14 days of the date on the notice | €30 |
| From day 15 | €60 |
| After a reminder, sent on day 29 | €75 |

You can pay:

- by bank transfer to the account on the notice, with your fine number as the reference
- by card on 01632 960000, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00
- by card at City Hall, Market Square 1

If you can’t pay all at once, call us. We can agree to let you pay in parts.

## Challenge a fine

You can challenge a fine if you think it’s wrong. For example:

- you had a valid permit or parking ticket
- the signs were missing or hard to read
- your car broke down, was stolen or had already been sold

Challenge it within 28 days of the date on the notice. Use the [contact form](/contact), or write to Parking team, City Hall, Market Square 1. Include:

- the fine number
- your name and address
- why you think the fine is wrong
- any proof, such as photos, your ticket or a garage bill

You don’t have to pay while we look at your challenge.

## What happens next

We reply in writing within 6 weeks. If we agree with you, we cancel the fine. If we don’t, you can still pay the lower amount of €30 within 14 days of our letter.

## If you disagree with our decision

You can appeal to the independent parking appeals panel within 28 days of our letter. It doesn’t cost anything, and the panel is not part of the council. Our letter tells you how to appeal. The panel’s decision is final.
