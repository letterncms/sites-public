We empty grey bins (rubbish) and blue bins (recycling) every week. Your collection day depends on the district you live in.

## Collection days by district

| District | Collection day |
| --- | --- |
| Old Town | Monday and Thursday |
| Riverside | Tuesday |
| Northgate | Wednesday |
| Heathfield | Thursday |
| Millbrook | Friday |
| Eastmoor | Monday |

In Old Town we collect twice a week, because many streets are narrow and most homes have no room for bins. Use the grey and blue sacks we give you and leave them at the collection point at the end of your street.

Green bins for garden waste are emptied every 2 weeks. You get your dates when you [order a garden waste bin](/services/get-a-garden-waste-bin).

## Putting your bins out

- Put your bins at the edge of the pavement by 7:00 on your collection day.
- Don’t put them out before 18:00 the evening before.
- Close the lid. We can’t empty a bin that is overfull or too heavy to tip.
- Take your bins back in by 20:00.

## Public holidays

When a public holiday falls on a weekday, every collection after it moves one day later that week. So in a week with a holiday on Monday, Friday’s collection happens on Saturday. The days change more over Christmas and New Year. We publish those changes in [news](/news) by early December.

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"tip","text":"From 4 January 2027 we also collect food waste every week on your usual day. Every home gets a kitchen caddy and a brown food bin by 18 December."}}
:::

## If we missed your bin

Check that your bin was out by 7:00 and the lid was closed. If it was, [report a missed bin](/report-a-problem) by the end of the next working day. We come back within 2 working days.

If your bin is broken or has gone missing, use the same form. We replace it free of charge within 10 working days.
