Children start primary school in the September after their 4th birthday. If your child was born between 1 September 2022 and 31 August 2023, apply between 1 November 2026 and 31 January 2027.

## How to apply

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Visit schools you are interested in. Most hold open mornings between November and January. Ask the school for dates.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Download the application form from [documents](/documents), or pick one up at City Hall or the Central Library.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Choose up to 3 schools, in the order you prefer them.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Send the form by 31 January 2027, by email to info@aldermoor.example or by post to City Hall, Market Square 1. We confirm we have it within 5 working days.
::::
:::

## How we decide

If a school has more applications than places, we give places in this order:

1. children who need that school for a medical or social reason, with a letter from a doctor or social worker
2. children with a brother or sister at the school in September 2027
3. children who live closest to the school, measured in a straight line from home to the school gate

Putting a school first doesn’t give your child a better chance at it. It only tells us which offer you would prefer.

## What happens next

We send offers by email and post on 15 April 2027. Accept your place by 29 April 2027. If you don’t get your first choice, we keep your child on the waiting list for the schools you liked better.

If you apply after 31 January, we look at your application after all the ones that came in on time.

## If you disagree

You can appeal within 20 school days of your offer letter. An independent appeals panel hears appeals in June. The letter explains how to appeal.

## Moving to Aldermoor during the school year?

You can apply for a place in any year group at any time. Use the same form, and we reply within 15 school days.
