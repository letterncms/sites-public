## Youth centres

| Centre | Address | Open |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Northgate Youth Centre | Station Road 62 | Monday to Friday 15:00 to 21:00 |
| Millbrook Youth Centre | Mill Street 21 | Tuesday to Saturday 14:00 to 21:00 |
| Eastmoor Youth Centre | Moor Lane 5 | Monday, Wednesday and Friday 15:00 to 22:00 |

You don’t need to sign up. Just come in. On your first visit, a youth worker shows you around and asks for your name and a phone number for a parent or carer.

Sessions until 18:00 are for ages 10 to 13. Evenings are for ages 14 to 18.

## What you can do

- get help with homework, Monday to Thursday 15:30 to 17:30
- make and record music in the studio
- play football, basketball and table tennis
- learn to cook and eat together on Friday evenings
- start your own project, from a podcast to a skate event

## Holiday activities

In every school holiday we run trips, sports days and workshops. We publish the programme on [events](/events) 3 weeks before each holiday. Book a place with the [contact form](/contact). Most activities are free. Trips cost up to €10, and they are free with an [Aldermoor Pass](/services/get-help-with-money-worries).

## Someone to talk to

Our youth workers are there to listen, about school, friends, home or anything else. What you say stays private. If they are worried about your safety, they will tell you before they talk to anyone else.
