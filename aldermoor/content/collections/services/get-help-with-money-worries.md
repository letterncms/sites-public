Many people in Aldermoor find it hard to make ends meet. You don’t have to sort it out alone, and you won’t be judged. Our help is free and confidential.

## Talk to a money adviser

A money adviser can help you:

- make a budget that works for you
- check you get all the benefits, allowances and reductions you are entitled to
- talk to the people you owe money to and agree payments you can afford
- deal with letters from debt collectors

Call 01632 960000 or use the [contact form](/contact), and an adviser calls you back within 2 working days.

## Meet an adviser in person

Bring any letters and bills you have.

- Central Library, Library Lane 2: drop in on Wednesday 13:00 to 16:00, no appointment needed
- Northgate Service Point, Station Road 40: Friday 9:00 to 13:00, by [appointment](/make-an-appointment)

## The Aldermoor Pass

The Aldermoor Pass is free. It gives you:

- half-price swimming at [Riverside Pool](/services/swim-at-riverside-pool)
- 1 free [bulky waste collection](/services/book-a-bulky-waste-collection) a year
- a [garden waste bin](/services/get-a-garden-waste-bin) for €30 a year
- free youth holiday trips, and lower prices at many sports clubs and at the city’s museums

You can get the pass if your household’s take-home income is no more than €1,550 a month if you live alone, or €2,200 for a couple. Add €300 for each child under 18. Ask an adviser, or apply at City Hall with your ID and bank statements for the last 3 months. The pass lasts 1 year.

## If you need help today

- If you have no money for food, we can refer you to a food bank the same day.
- If you are behind with rent, see [help paying your rent](/services/get-help-paying-your-rent).
- If your council tax is too high for you, apply for a [council tax reduction](/services/get-a-council-tax-reduction).
