The Central Library is at Library Lane 2. Since 2 November 2026 we are open longer, and on Sundays too.

| Day | Opening hours |
| --- | --- |
| Monday to Thursday | 10:00 to 20:00 |
| Friday | 10:00 to 18:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00 to 17:00 |
| Sunday | 12:00 to 17:00 |

## How to join

Joining is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in Aldermoor, at any age.

Come to the front desk with your ID card or passport. If it doesn’t show an Aldermoor address, bring a letter with your name and address on it, such as a bank statement, or proof that you work or study here. A parent or carer signs for children under 12. You get your card straight away.

## What you can borrow

- up to 20 items at a time, for 3 weeks
- more than 120,000 books, films, magazines and board games
- e-books and audiobooks, through the library app, with your card number and PIN

You can renew items twice, at the desk or in the app, unless someone else has reserved them.

We don’t charge late fees. If you keep an item more than 6 weeks after it was due, we ask you to pay for a new one. If you bring it back later, we cancel the charge.

## Also at the library

- free Wi-Fi, 40 free computers, and printing for €0.10 a page
- study rooms for 1 to 8 people, free to book
- help with computers, phones and online services, every weekday from 10:00 to 12:00
- [money advice](/services/get-help-with-money-worries), Wednesday 13:00 to 16:00
- storytelling on the children’s floor every Wednesday and Saturday, and a monthly repair café: see [events](/events)

The library is step-free, with lifts to every floor, a Changing Places toilet with a hoist, and screen readers and magnifiers on 4 computers.
