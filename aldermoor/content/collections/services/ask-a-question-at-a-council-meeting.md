The city council meets once a month, on a Thursday at 19:30, in the Council Chamber at City Hall. Every meeting is open to the public. You can also watch it live, or later, on the [council](/council) page. Meeting dates are on that page, and the agenda is in [documents](/documents) 10 days before each meeting.

## Ask a question

You can ask about anything the council is responsible for.

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Write your question in no more than 100 words.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Send it with the [contact form](/contact) by 12:00 on the Monday before the meeting. Say if you want to read it out yourself.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
We confirm by email that your question is on the list.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
At the meeting, you or the chair reads the question out, and the mayor or a deputy mayor answers it.
::::
:::

There are 30 minutes for questions at each meeting. If we run out of time, we send you a written answer within 10 working days and publish it on the [council](/council) page.

## Speak about an item on the agenda

You can speak for up to 3 minutes before councillors debate an item. Up to 5 people can speak on each item. Sign up the same way, by 12:00 on the Monday before. Tell us which item and whether you are for or against the proposal.

## Questions we can’t take

- questions about a named person, such as a member of staff
- cases that are in court, or planning decisions that are still open to objection
- offensive or discriminatory language

If we can’t take your question, we tell you why and suggest another way to raise it.

## On the evening

Doors open at 19:00, through the main entrance on Market Square. The public gallery has 80 seats, 6 places for wheelchair users and a hearing loop. If you need other support, tell us when you sign up.

There are other ways to have a say too. Answer our consultations on [have your say](/have-your-say), or contact your local councillors through the [council](/council) page.
