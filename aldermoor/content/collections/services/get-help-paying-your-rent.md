Contact us as soon as you think you might fall behind. The earlier you tell us, the more we can do. Many people we help are in work, and it’s never too early to ask.

## How we can help

- talk to your landlord and agree a payment plan you can afford
- check you get all the benefits and allowances you are entitled to
- give you a one-off grant of up to €1,500 from our Housing Support Fund to clear rent arrears, if it stops you losing your home
- lend you money for a deposit, without interest, if you need to move somewhere cheaper
- arrange [free help with money worries](/services/get-help-with-money-worries), if you have other debts too

## What you need

When we meet, bring as much of this as you can:

- your rental contract
- bank statements for the last 3 months
- payslips or letters about benefits
- any letters from your landlord about the money you owe

Don’t worry if you don’t have everything. We can start without it.

## What happens next

We call you back within 2 working days and meet you within 5 working days, at City Hall or in your home. If you ask for a grant, we decide within 10 working days. We pay it straight to your landlord.
