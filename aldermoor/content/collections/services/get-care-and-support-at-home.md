If daily life has become hard because of age, illness or a disability, we can help you stay independent at home. You can also ask for someone else, with their permission.

## Help we can arrange

- help with washing, dressing and getting up
- help with cleaning
- meals delivered to your home
- equipment and changes to your home, such as grab rails, a ramp, a stairlift or a walk-in shower
- day centres, to meet people and get out of the house
- a personal alarm, to call for help at any time
- a break for the person who cares for you

## How it works

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Call 01632 960000 or use the [contact form](/contact). Tell us what has become hard.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
A care adviser visits you at home within 2 weeks, or within 2 working days if it is urgent. You can have a family member or friend with you.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Together you agree what help you need. We send you the plan in writing within 2 weeks of the visit.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Your help starts. We check at least once a year that it is still right, and sooner if your needs change.
::::
:::

## What it costs

For most help you pay a contribution of up to €21 every 4 weeks, however much help you get. If your income is very low, you pay nothing. The visit, advice and support for carers are always free.

For big changes, such as a stairlift, you may pay part of the cost, depending on your income and savings. We tell you before anything is ordered.
