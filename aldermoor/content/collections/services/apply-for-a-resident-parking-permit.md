## Permit zones

There are 2 permit zones:

- **Zone A**: Old Town, inside the ring road
- **Zone B**: Riverside, between Alder Quay and the railway line

In these zones, parking is for permit holders or paid at the meter, Monday to Saturday 8:00 to 22:00. On Sundays and public holidays anyone can park free.

## Prices

| Permit | Price |
| --- | --- |
| First car | €85 a year |
| Second car | €170 a year |
| Visitor parking | €1.50 a day, up to 60 days a year |

You can have up to 2 permits for each household. Visitor days can be used for any car. You add the number plate yourself by phone or by email.

## What you need

- your address in the zone, registered with us (if you have just moved, [tell us you have moved](/services/tell-us-you-have-moved) first)
- the vehicle registration document in your name, or a letter from your employer or lease company if it’s a company or lease car
- your number plate

## How to apply

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Email info@aldermoor.example with “parking permit” as the subject. Include your name, address and number plate, and attach a photo or scan of your vehicle registration document.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Or bring your documents to City Hall, Market Square 1. You don’t need an appointment.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
We check your address and car, then email you a payment link.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Your permit starts as soon as you have paid, usually within 5 working days.
::::
:::

Your permit is digital and linked to your number plate. You don’t need to put anything in your windscreen. Parking attendants check the plate.

## If something changes

If you get a new car, or move to another street in the same zone, email us and we move your permit free of charge. If you move out of the zone, we refund every full month you have left.
