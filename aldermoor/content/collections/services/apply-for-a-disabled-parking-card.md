With a disabled parking card you can:

- park in any disabled parking bay in Aldermoor
- park free at parking meters, and for as long as you need
- park for up to 3 hours where there is a time limit
- ask for a personal parking bay near your home

The card belongs to you, not to a car. You can use it when you drive or when someone drives you.

## Who can get a card

You can apply if you live in Aldermoor and one of these is true:

- you can’t walk more than 100 metres without stopping or help, because of a condition that will last at least 6 months
- you use a wheelchair all the time
- you are blind or partially sighted
- you are the parent of a child aged 3 or over who meets one of the points above

## What you need

- your ID card or passport
- a recent passport photo
- letters from your doctor or specialist about your condition, if you have them

## What happens next

We may ask you to see an independent doctor. This is free, takes about 30 minutes and happens at City Hall. If you can’t travel, the doctor can visit you at home.

We decide within 8 weeks and send the card by post. It is valid for 5 years. We write to you 8 weeks before it runs out.

## A parking bay for you

If you have a card and a car but no driveway, you can ask for a parking bay outside your home. It is free and shows your number plate, so only your car can use it. It takes about 12 weeks to set up.
