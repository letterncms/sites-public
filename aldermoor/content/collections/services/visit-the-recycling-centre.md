Heathfield Recycling Centre, Quarry Road 14, is open Tuesday to Saturday 9:00 to 17:00. The last car goes in at 16:45. We are closed on Sunday, Monday and public holidays. Weekday afternoons are the quietest time.

## What to bring with you

The centre is for Aldermoor residents only. Bring your ID card or driving licence with your Aldermoor address, or a council tax bill in your name.

## What you can bring for free

- paper, card, glass, plastic, cans and cartons
- garden waste, wood and metal
- electrical items, batteries and light bulbs
- small amounts of paint, oil and household chemicals, at the chemicals counter
- clothes, books and furniture in good condition, for the reuse corner
- mattresses, carpets and other large items

There is no limit for recycling. Staff can help you unload heavy items: ask at the gate.

## What you pay for

| Waste | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Building rubble, bricks and tiles, over 1 cubic metre | €15 |
| Car tyres | €5 each |
| Asbestos, only if you book first | €25 |

Up to 1 cubic metre of rubble is free. That is about a full car boot with the back seats folded down. You pay by card at the gate. To bring asbestos, call 01632 960000 first, and we will tell you how to wrap it safely.

## Cars, vans and trailers

Cars and small vans up to 3.5 tonnes are welcome. Trailers up to 2 metres long are fine. Larger vehicles need a business permit, because the centre is not for business waste.
