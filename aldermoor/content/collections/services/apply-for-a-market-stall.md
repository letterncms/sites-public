The market on Market Square runs every Wednesday and Saturday from 8:00 to 17:00, with about 90 stalls.

## Pitches and prices

| Pitch | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Day pitch, 3 by 3 metres | €28 a day |
| Fixed pitch, the same spot every market day | €38 a market day, billed every month |
| Electricity | €6 a day |

There is a waiting list for fixed pitches. At the moment it is about 8 months for food stalls and 3 months for other stalls. Day pitches are usually available within 2 weeks.

## What you need

- your business registration number
- liability insurance that covers at least €1 million
- for food: a food hygiene certificate and your food business registration
- a photo or drawing of your stall and a short description of what you sell

## Market rules

- Set up from 6:30. All vehicles must be off the square by 8:00.
- Clear your pitch by 18:00 and take your rubbish with you.
- Don’t play music through speakers.
- Show your prices clearly.

If you can’t come, tell the supervisor by 7:00 the day before. If you miss 4 market days in a row without telling us, we can give your fixed pitch to the next trader on the list.

## The winter market and the Market Square plan

The winter market has its own selection. Applications for 2027 open on 1 May 2027. We are also making a new plan for Market Square, which may change the market layout from 2028. Tell us what you think on [have your say](/have-your-say).
