Tell us within 5 days of moving. We use your address to send your council tax bill, your polling card and letters about school places.

## Moving within Aldermoor

Email info@aldermoor.example with:

- the names and dates of birth of everyone who is moving
- your old and new address
- the date you moved
- a copy of your rental contract or the deed of your new home

If you move in with someone, send a letter signed by the main occupant to say you live there, with a copy of their ID instead.

We confirm your new address by email within 5 working days.

## Moving to Aldermoor from another town

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
[Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) at City Hall. You can book up to 4 weeks before you move.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Bring an ID card or passport for each person, and proof of your new address.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
If you are coming from another country, also bring your birth certificate, and your marriage or partnership certificate if you have one. If they are in another language, bring an official translation.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
We register you during the appointment, which takes about 20 minutes.
::::
:::

## Leaving Aldermoor

If you move to another town in this country, register with your new council. They will tell us. If you move abroad, email us up to 5 days before you leave.

## What we update for you

When we have your new address, we update your council tax, the electoral register and your child’s school place details. You still need to tell your bank, doctor, employer and energy supplier yourself.

If you have a [resident parking permit](/services/apply-for-a-resident-parking-permit), email the parking team separately. Your permit doesn’t move with your address automatically.
