If life is in danger, call 112.

## Get flood alerts

We send a free text message when the River Alder is likely to flood. Homes most at risk are in Riverside, the Old Town streets along the quay and the low parts of Millbrook. To sign up, call 01632 960000 or use the [contact form](/contact). Give us your mobile number and your address.

## Make an emergency kit

Keep these together in a bag that is easy to grab:

- drinking water: 3 litres for each person for each day, for 3 days
- food that keeps and needs no cooking
- a torch and a radio that work on batteries, and spare batteries
- medicines you need every day
- copies of important documents in a waterproof bag
- a charged power bank and some cash

## If we send a flood warning

- Move valuables, documents and medicines upstairs.
- Pick up free sandbags, up to 10 for each home, at Heathfield Recycling Centre. In Riverside we bring sandbags by truck: the alert says where.
- If water comes into your home, switch off the gas and electricity, if it is safe to do so.
- Check on neighbours who might need help.

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"warning","text":"Never walk or drive through floodwater. 15 cm of fast water can knock you over, and 30 cm can float a car."}}
:::

## If you hear the sirens

We test the sirens on the first Monday of every month at 12:00. At any other time, a siren means there is a danger, such as a fire with toxic smoke.

1. Go inside and close all doors and windows.
2. Switch off fans and ventilation.
3. Follow the news on local radio or [our news page](/news).

## If you need extra help

If you would struggle to leave your home in an emergency, because of illness, disability or age, tell us. We keep a list of people who need extra help, so emergency services can reach you first.
