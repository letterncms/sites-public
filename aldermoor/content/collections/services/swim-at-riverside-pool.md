Riverside Pool, Alder Quay 8, reopened on 24 October 2026 after a full renovation. It has a 25-metre pool with 6 lanes, a learner pool with a floor that moves up and down, and new changing rooms.

## Opening hours

The pool is open Monday to Friday 7:00 to 21:30, Saturday 8:00 to 18:00 and Sunday 9:00 to 16:00.

| Session | When |
| --- | --- |
| Lane swimming | Monday to Friday 7:00 to 9:00 and 19:30 to 21:30 |
| Family swim, with inflatables | Saturday 10:00 to 12:00 |
| Quiet swim, with no music and low lighting | Sunday 9:00 to 10:00 |

At other times there is space for everyone, with at least 2 lanes kept for lengths.

## Prices

| Ticket | Adult | Child aged 4 to 15 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Single swim | €5.50 | €3 |
| 10-swim card | €45 | €25 |

Children under 4 swim free. With an [Aldermoor Pass](/services/get-help-with-money-worries), you pay half. A carer who comes with a disabled swimmer goes in free. Pay by card at the door.

## Swimming lessons

We teach children from 4 years old, in small groups on weekday afternoons, and adults of any age in the evenings. Lessons run in blocks of 10 weeks, and a block costs €65. Put your name on the list with the [contact form](/contact). The wait for children’s lessons is about 3 months at the moment.

## Getting in and around

- The entrance and both pools are step-free.
- Both pools have a hoist, and there is a ramp into the learner pool.
- There is a fully accessible changing room with a hoist and an adult-sized changing bench.
- Assistance dogs are welcome in the reception area and on the poolside viewing balcony.

Children under 8 must have an adult with them in the water. One adult can bring up to 2 children under 8.
