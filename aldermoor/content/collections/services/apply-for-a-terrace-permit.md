You need a terrace permit to put tables, chairs, umbrellas or heaters on a pavement or square.

## The rules

- Keep at least 1.8 metres of pavement free, so people with wheelchairs, prams and white canes can pass easily.
- Your terrace can be open until 23:00 from Sunday to Thursday, and until midnight on Friday and Saturday.
- After closing, bring the furniture inside, or stack it and lock it to your front wall.
- Don’t use gas heaters. Electric heaters are allowed.
- Don’t play music outside.
- In Old Town, furniture and umbrellas must be in plain colours, without advertising.

## Prices

| Where | Price per m² |
| --- | --- |
| Old Town, all year | €42 a year |
| Other districts, all year | €28 a year |
| Summer only, 1 April to 31 October | 60% of the yearly price |

For example, a 12 m² terrace in Old Town, open all year, costs €504 a year.

## Apply for a permit

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Draw your terrace with measurements: show the building, the pavement, the free space for walking and any trees, bins or lamp posts nearby.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Add photos of the place and of your furniture, and a copy of your liability insurance.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Email it to info@aldermoor.example, or bring it to City Hall, Market Square 1.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
We visit the place and ask your direct neighbours for their views. We decide within 6 weeks.
::::
:::

Your permit stays valid as long as nothing changes. We send you a bill every January.
