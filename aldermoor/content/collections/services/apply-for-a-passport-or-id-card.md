Everyone needs to come in person, children too. We check your photo and take fingerprints from age 12. [Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) at City Hall.

## Fees

| Document | Age 18 and over | Under 18 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Passport | €75 | €55 |
| ID card | €45 | €30 |
| Fast service, ready the next working day | €50 extra | €50 extra |

Passports and ID cards for adults are valid for 10 years. For under 18s they are valid for 5 years. You pay by card when you apply.

## What to bring

- your current passport or ID card, even if it has expired
- 1 colour photo, taken in the last 6 months, against a plain light background
- for a child under 18: a consent form signed by the parent who isn’t coming, and a copy of their ID. Download the form from [documents](/documents)

If your passport or ID card was stolen, bring a police report. If you lost it, we fill in a form with you at your appointment.

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"tip","text":"Apply well before you travel. In May and June, appointments are often booked up 4 weeks ahead."}}
:::

## Collecting your document

We text you when it is ready, usually after 6 working days. Collect it within 3 months at City Hall, without an appointment. Or [make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) to collect it at Northgate Service Point on a Tuesday or Friday morning. Bring your old passport or ID card, and we will make it invalid.

A parent collects a document for a child under 18. Adults collect their own.

## Lost it or had it stolen?

Call 01632 960000 as soon as you can, so we can block the document and no one else can use it. If it turns up later, you can’t use it anymore. Bring it to City Hall and we will destroy it safely.
