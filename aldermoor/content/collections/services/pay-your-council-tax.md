Council tax pays for many of our services, from bin collections to street lights. We send the bill for the year at the end of February.

## What is on your bill

Your bill has up to 2 parts:

- **Property tax**, if you own your home. It is 0.09% of the value of your home. We work out the value every year, and it is printed on your bill.
- **Waste charge**, for every household, whether you rent or own. In 2027 it is €312 a year if you live alone and €398 a year for 2 or more people.

## Ways to pay

- **Direct debit**: we take the money in 10 equal parts, on the 28th of each month from March to December. To set it up, call 01632 960000 or fill in the form on your bill.
- **Bank transfer**: pay the full amount by 31 March. Use the payment reference on your bill.
- **At City Hall**: pay by card at Market Square 1, Monday to Friday 8:30 to 17:00, and until 20:00 on Thursday.

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"tip","text":"If your income is low, you may pay less. Find out if you can <a href=\"/services/get-a-council-tax-reduction\">get a council tax reduction</a>."}}
:::

## If your bill is wrong

If the number of people in your home is wrong, call us and we will send a new bill.

If you think the value of your home is too high, object within 6 weeks of the date on your bill. Tell us why with the [contact form](/contact). We decide within 8 weeks. Keep paying while we look at it: if you are right, we refund the difference.

## If you can’t pay

Contact us before the payment is due. We can agree a payment plan of up to 12 months.

If you don’t pay and don’t contact us, we send a reminder after 14 days, which adds €7.50 to your bill. After that, we can take the money from your wages or bank account. We would much rather agree a plan with you.
