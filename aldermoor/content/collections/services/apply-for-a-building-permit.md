## When you need a permit

You need a building permit to:

- build an extension, a dormer window or an extra floor
- change the front of a building, such as windows, doors or a shopfront
- turn a house into flats, or a shop into a home
- take down a building or a wall that holds up part of it

In the Old Town conservation area you also need a permit for almost any change people can see from the street, such as new window frames or roof tiles.

## When you don’t need one

- a shed or summer house in your back garden, smaller than 10 m² and lower than 3 metres
- work inside that doesn’t change the structure, such as a new kitchen or bathroom
- solar panels on a back roof, outside the Old Town

If you are not sure, book a free 30-minute advice meeting before you spend money on drawings.

## What happens next

We publish a short notice about your plans, so neighbours know. We decide within 8 weeks. For larger plans we may need 6 weeks more, and we will tell you if so. Don’t start work before you have the permit.

The fee is 2.4% of the building costs, with a minimum of €180. For an extension that costs €40,000, you pay €960. We send the bill with our decision. If we refuse the permit, you pay half.

## If you disagree

You, or anyone directly affected, can object in writing within 6 weeks of the date of our decision. An independent committee looks at your objection before we decide again.
