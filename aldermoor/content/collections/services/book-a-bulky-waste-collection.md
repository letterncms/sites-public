## What we collect

We take things that are too big for your bin, such as:

- sofas, chairs, tables, wardrobes, beds and mattresses
- fridges, freezers, washing machines and cookers
- carpets and rugs, rolled up and tied
- bikes, garden furniture and large toys

We don’t take building rubble, plasterboard, tyres, car parts, gas bottles, paint or chemicals. You can bring most of these to [Heathfield Recycling Centre](/services/visit-the-recycling-centre).

## Prices

| Number of items | Price |
| --- | --- |
| Up to 5 items | €25 |
| Each extra item, up to 10 in total | €5 |

Each piece counts as one item, so a bed frame and its mattress are 2 items. If you have an [Aldermoor Pass](/services/get-help-with-money-worries), your first collection each year is free.

## On the day

- Put your items outside your front door or at the edge of the pavement by 7:00.
- Leave at least 1.5 metres of pavement clear for wheelchairs and prams.
- We only take the items you booked. Anything else stays behind.

We don’t normally go inside homes. If you can’t carry items out because of age, illness or disability, tell us when you book. Our crew will collect them from inside at no extra cost.

## Change or cancel a booking

You can change the date or cancel up to 2 working days before your collection. We refund the full price. Call 01632 960000 or reply to your confirmation email.

## Book a collection

Fill in the form below and pay by card. We email you a confirmation with your collection date, usually within 10 working days. If you can’t use the form, call 01632 960000, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00.
