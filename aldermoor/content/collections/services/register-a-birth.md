Register the birth within 5 days. If the 5th day is a weekend or public holiday, you can come on the next working day. [Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) at City Hall. We keep slots free every day for birth registrations, so you won’t have to wait long.

## Who can register

Either parent can register the birth. If neither parent can come, someone who was there at the birth can do it.

## What to bring

- your ID card or passport
- the ID of the other parent, if they are coming too
- the birth notice you got from the hospital or midwife
- the first name or names you have chosen

If you are married or in a registered partnership, you are both recorded as parents straight away. If you are not, the other parent can recognise the child at the registration. You both need to come, with your IDs. You can also do this before the birth, by appointment.

## Your child’s surname

If you are married or in a registered partnership, you choose together which surname your child gets. All your children together then get the same surname. We explain the options at the appointment.

## What happens next

We give you 1 free copy of the birth certificate. Extra copies cost €14 each. We add your baby to the population register at your address, so you don’t need to [tell us your baby has moved in](/services/tell-us-you-have-moved). Your child gets a personal number, which you need for health care and child benefit.
