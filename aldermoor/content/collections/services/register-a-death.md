We are sorry for your loss. This page explains what needs to happen, and where you can get help.

A death in Aldermoor must be registered within 5 days. In most cases the funeral director does this for you. Ask them first.

## If you register the death yourself

[Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) at City Hall. Bring:

- the medical certificate of the cause of death, from the doctor
- the ID card or passport of the person who died, if you have it
- your own ID card or passport

The appointment takes about 30 minutes. You can bring someone with you for support.

## Death certificates

We give you 1 free copy of the death certificate. Extra copies cost €14 each. Banks, insurers and pension providers often ask for one, so you may want 2 or 3.

The funeral or cremation can go ahead once the death has been registered.

## We tell other council teams for you

When we register a death, we tell our council tax, housing, care, parking and library teams. You don’t have to call each of them. We stop letters in the name of the person who died within 10 working days.

## Help with funeral costs

If the person who died had no money and no family who can pay, we can arrange a simple and dignified funeral. Call 01632 960000 before you sign anything with a funeral director.

## Support for you

If you are worried about money after a death, our advisers can help you sort out bills and benefits. Find out more about [help with money worries](/services/get-help-with-money-worries). If you were caring for the person who died, our care advisers can also talk with you about what happens next.
