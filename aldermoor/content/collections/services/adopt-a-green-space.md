You can adopt a small piece of public green space near your home: a tree pit, a verge, a planter or a corner of a park. You decide what to grow, from wildflowers for bees to herbs your neighbours can pick.

More than 300 spaces across Aldermoor are already looked after by residents.

## What we give you

- plants, bulbs and seeds worth up to €250 a year for each space
- tools, gloves and litter pickers to borrow
- a free delivery of compost once a year
- insurance while you are working in your space
- a small sign with the name of your group, if you want one

## What we ask of you

- look after your space at least once a month from March to October
- don’t use weedkiller or other chemicals
- keep paths clear, and keep plants lower than 60 cm near road junctions so drivers can see
- tell us if you want to stop, so someone else can take over

## Meet other green volunteers

Twice a year we hold a meeting for everyone who looks after a space, to swap seeds and ideas. We invite you by email.
