You can report a problem in any street, square, path or park in Aldermoor. You don’t need to live here.

## What you can report

- potholes and broken pavements
- street lights that are out, or that stay on during the day
- litter, overflowing litter bins and dog mess
- graffiti and posters on walls
- fly-tipping: rubbish dumped in the street or a park
- missed bin collections
- broken benches, signs and play equipment
- blocked drains and flooded paths

## How to report a problem

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Go to [report a problem](/report-a-problem).
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Show us where it is on the map, or give the street and nearest house number.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Tell us what the problem is. Add a photo if you can: it helps our teams bring the right tools.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Leave your email address if you want updates. You can also report without giving your name.
::::
:::

We send you a reference number. You get an email when we have fixed the problem, or if it’s going to take longer than planned.

## How quickly we fix things

| Problem | We aim to fix it within |
| --- | --- |
| Something dangerous, such as an open drain cover | 24 hours |
| Offensive graffiti | 24 hours |
| Missed bin | 2 working days |
| Fly-tipping | 3 working days |
| Potholes | 5 working days |
| Street lights and other graffiti | 10 working days |

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"warning","text":"If something is dangerous right now, call 01632 960000 and choose option 1. This line takes urgent calls about roads, trees and flooding 24 hours a day. If someone is hurt, call 112."}}
:::

## Things we don’t look after

Some things belong to others, such as bus and tram shelters, power cables and private car parks. If you report one of these, we pass it on and tell you who is dealing with it. If you smell gas, leave the area and call 112.
