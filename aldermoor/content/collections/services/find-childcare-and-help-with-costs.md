## Types of childcare

- **Day nurseries** look after babies and children up to 4, usually 7:30 to 18:30 on weekdays.
- **Childminders** look after a small group of children in their own home.
- **Pre-school playgroups** are for children from 2 and a half to 4. They run in the mornings and help children get ready for school.
- **Out-of-school care** is for children aged 4 to 12, before and after school and in the holidays.

We inspect every registered childcare provider at least once a year. You can find the list of providers and their latest inspection reports in [documents](/documents).

Many day nurseries have a waiting list of 6 months or more. Put your name down early, even before your baby is born.

## Free pre-school hours

Every child aged 2 and a half to 4 can go to a pre-school playgroup for 16 hours a week for free, 40 weeks a year. You don’t need to apply to us: ask the playgroup.

## Help with costs

If you work, study or follow a job programme, we pay part of the cost of registered childcare. How much depends on your household income.

| Household income per year, before tax | We pay |
| --- | --- |
| Up to €30,000 | 90% |
| €30,001 to €50,000 | 70% |
| €50,001 to €80,000 | 45% |
| More than €80,000 | 30% |

We pay up to €10.25 an hour for a day nursery and up to €8.75 an hour for out-of-school care. Apply within 3 months of your child starting. The form is in [documents](/documents). We pay you every month.
