A marriage and a registered partnership give you almost the same rights. The ceremony is the same too. You can choose whichever suits you.

## Give notice

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Choose a date and a type of ceremony. You can book up to 1 year ahead.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
[Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) at City Hall to give notice together. Do this at least 2 weeks and at most 1 year before the ceremony.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Bring your ID cards or passports. If you were born abroad, bring your birth certificate. If you were married before, bring proof of your divorce or your partner’s death certificate.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Give us the names of your 2 to 4 witnesses, aged 18 or over, with a copy of their ID.
::::
:::

## Ceremonies and prices

| Ceremony | When | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short ceremony in the registry office, up to 10 guests | Tuesday at 9:00 or 9:30 | Free |
| Ceremony in the Wedding Room at City Hall, up to 80 guests | Monday to Friday | €280 |
| Ceremony in the Wedding Room on a Saturday | Saturday 10:00 to 16:00 | €420 |
| Ceremony at another approved place in Aldermoor | Any day | €560 |

You can find the list of approved places in [documents](/documents). You pay when you give notice.

## On the day

Arrive 15 minutes early. Your witnesses need to bring their ID. Our registrar leads the ceremony, which lasts about 20 minutes in the Wedding Room. You can choose music and add your own words or a reading.

After the ceremony you sign the certificate with your witnesses. You get 1 free copy.

## Change or cancel

You can change the date or cancel free of charge up to 4 weeks before. After that, we keep €50 to cover our costs.

## Changing your name

Getting married or registering a partnership doesn’t change your surname in the register. You can choose to use your partner’s name in daily life, and we can show it on your passport or ID card.
