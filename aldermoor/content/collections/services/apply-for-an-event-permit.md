You need to tell us about any event in a street, square or park. What you need depends on its size.

## Which permit you need

| Event | Apply at least | Price |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small: under 250 people, no road closure and no amplified music after 22:00 | 4 weeks before | Free |
| Street party for neighbours, with a road closure | 6 weeks before | Free |
| Medium: 250 to 2,000 people | 12 weeks before | €95 |
| Large: more than 2,000 people | 26 weeks before | €450 |

## What to send us

- the event form, from [documents](/documents)
- a site plan showing stages, stalls, toilets, exits and first aid
- a copy of your liability insurance
- how you will deal with rubbish and keep the place clean
- for medium and large events: a safety plan and the name of your safety officer

Send it all to info@aldermoor.example.

## What happens next

We share your plans with the police, fire service and ambulance service. We may ask you to change things, such as where the exits are or how late the music plays. For a medium or large event, we usually meet you on site.

We tell you our decision at least 2 weeks before a small event, and at least 4 weeks before a medium or large one. You must write to the neighbours about your event at least 2 weeks before it takes place.

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"warning","text":"Don’t sell tickets or announce your event before you have a permit. If we can’t give a permit in time, you may have to refund everyone."}}
:::

## Street parties

Street parties make neighbours into friends, and we want to make them easy. Apply 6 weeks before, and make sure most people in the street agree. We lend you road closure signs and barriers for free. Collect them from Heathfield Recycling Centre the day before and bring them back the day after.
