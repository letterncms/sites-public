We look after about 46,000 trees in streets and parks. We plant new ones every winter and check every tree at least once every 3 years.

## Ask for a new tree

You can ask for a tree in your street or in a green space near you. Use the [contact form](/contact) and tell us where.

We check whether there is room for the roots and the crown, and whether there are cables or pipes under the ground. We tell you within 8 weeks if we can plant a tree there. We plant from November to March. If you ask by 30 September, we plant in the coming winter.

This winter we are planting 600 new street trees, most of them in Eastmoor and Northgate.

## Ask us to prune a tree

We prune a council tree when:

- it blocks a street light, a traffic sign or a window
- branches hang lower than 4.5 metres above a road or 2.5 metres above a pavement
- branches touch a building
- it has dead or broken branches

We inspect within 15 working days. We don’t prune or remove healthy trees because of falling leaves, sap, bird droppings or shade. Trees keep our streets cool and clean the air, so we keep as many as we can.

## Trees on private land

Trees in your own garden are your responsibility. To cut down a tree with a trunk more than 30 cm across, measured 1.3 metres above the ground, you need a felling permit. It costs €65, and we decide within 8 weeks.
