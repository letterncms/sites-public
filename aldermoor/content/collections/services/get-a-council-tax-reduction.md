We can cancel all or part of the waste charge and property tax on your [council tax bill](/services/pay-your-council-tax) if your income is low.

## Who can get a reduction

You can get a reduction if the take-home income of your household is no more than:

| Household | Income per month, after tax |
| --- | --- |
| You live alone | €1,550 |
| You live with a partner | €2,200 |
| For each child under 18 | add €300 |

Your savings must also be less than €8,000 if you live alone, or €12,000 for a couple. We don’t count the value of the home you live in.

These limits are the same as for the [Aldermoor Pass](/services/get-help-with-money-worries). If you get a reduction, you can get the pass too.

## How to apply

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Download the application form from [documents](/documents), or pick one up at City Hall or the Central Library.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Fill it in for everyone in your home aged 18 or over.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Add bank statements for all your accounts for the last 3 months, and your payslips or benefit letters.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
Email it to info@aldermoor.example, or post it to City Hall, Market Square 1. Do this within 3 months of the date on your bill.
::::
:::

## What happens next

We decide within 8 weeks. While we look at your application, we don’t ask you to pay. If you get a reduction, we send you a new bill.

Next year, we check your income again ourselves. If nothing has changed, you get the reduction without applying. We write to tell you either way.

## If you disagree

If you think our decision is wrong, write to us within 6 weeks of the date on our letter. Tell us why, and add any papers we didn’t see. Someone who wasn’t involved in the first decision looks at it again.
