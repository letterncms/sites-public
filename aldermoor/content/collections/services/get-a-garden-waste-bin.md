A garden waste bin is a green bin that holds 240 litres. It’s for grass cuttings, leaves, hedge trimmings, weeds, flowers and small branches up to 10 cm thick.

Don’t put in food, soil, stones, pet waste or plastic bags. We can’t compost them, and we will leave the bin unemptied.

## Prices for 2027

| Bin | Price |
| --- | --- |
| First green bin | €65 a year |
| Each extra green bin | €45 a year |
| With an Aldermoor Pass | €30 a year |

The year runs from 1 January to 31 December. If you join after 30 June, you pay half the price. Find out about the [Aldermoor Pass](/services/get-help-with-money-worries).

## When we empty it

We empty green bins every 2 weeks from March to November, and once a month from December to February. When you join, we email you a calendar with your dates. Put the bin at the edge of the pavement by 7:00 on the day.

## Renew or cancel

We send you a reminder to renew in January. If you haven’t renewed by 28 February, we collect the bin.

If you move out of Aldermoor, tell us and we refund the full months you have left. We don’t give refunds for other reasons once the year has started.

## Other ways to deal with garden waste

You can take garden waste to [Heathfield Recycling Centre](/services/visit-the-recycling-centre) for free. Or compost it at home: the recycling centre sells compost bins for €15.
