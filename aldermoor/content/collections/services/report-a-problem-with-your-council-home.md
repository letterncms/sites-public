If you rent your home from the council, we are responsible for most repairs to the building and the things that come with it.

## How to report a repair

Call 01632 960000, Monday to Friday 8:00 to 18:00. For an emergency outside these hours, call the same number and choose option 1.

For repairs that aren’t urgent, you can also use the [contact form](/contact). Tell us:

- your address and a phone number
- what is wrong and in which room
- when you are usually at home

A photo helps us bring the right parts the first time.

## How quickly we come

| Type of repair | Examples | We come within |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Emergency | No heating or hot water between October and April, a big leak, no electricity, a front door that won’t lock | 24 hours |
| Urgent | A blocked toilet if you only have one, a broken window, a leak you can catch in a bucket | 3 working days |
| Routine | A dripping tap, a sticking door, loose tiles | 20 working days |

We give you a time slot of 4 hours, and we text you when we are on our way.

## Damp and mould

Tell us as soon as you see it. Damp and mould can make people ill, especially children and older people. We inspect within 10 working days and tell you in writing what we will do and when. If someone in your home has a lung condition, tell us, and we will come sooner.

## Repairs that are yours

You look after small things inside your home, such as light bulbs, toilet seats and door handles. If you or a visitor damage something, we can still repair it, but we will charge you.

## If you’re not happy

If a repair isn’t done on time or isn’t done well, use the [contact form](/contact) to make a complaint. We reply within 10 working days.
