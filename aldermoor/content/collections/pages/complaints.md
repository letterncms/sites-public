If you are unhappy with a service, a decision or the way you were treated, tell us. Complaining doesn’t affect the service you get from us, and we use what you tell us to do better.

::: box {"props":{"tag":"ol"},"class":"steps"}
:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
**Tell the team involved.** Most problems are solved quickly by the people who know your case. Reply to their letter or email, or call 01632 960000.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
**Make a formal complaint.** If that didn’t help, use the [contact form](/contact) and start your message with “Complaint”. Tell us what happened, when, and what you would like us to do.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
**We investigate.** Someone who wasn’t involved looks into your complaint. We reply within 6 weeks, and tell you if we need longer.
::::

:::: box {"props":{"tag":"li"}}
**Ask the ombudsman.** If you are still unhappy, you can ask the independent national ombudsman to look at your complaint, free of charge.
::::
:::

## Help with your complaint

You can complain in any way that suits you: in writing, by phone, or at City Hall. Someone else can complain for you, such as a relative, a friend or an adviser, if you agree.

## What a complaint is not

A complaint is not the way to challenge a decision that has its own appeal procedure, such as a parking fine or a refused permit. The letter about the decision tells you how to object and by when.
