The full council meets once a month on a Thursday at 19:30, in the council chamber at City Hall. Meetings are public: anyone can come and listen, and every meeting is streamed live and recorded.

## Meetings in 2026 and 2027

| Date | Main subjects |
| --- | --- |
| 17 December 2026 | Market Square designs, winter support for residents |
| 21 January 2027 | Quiet streets trial in Millbrook |
| 18 February 2027 | Housing strategy: first year |
| 18 March 2027 | Climate plan progress |

The agenda is published 10 days before each meeting, and the minutes within a week after it, on the [documents page](/documents).

## Come to a meeting

The public gallery has 80 seats, 6 places for wheelchair users and a hearing loop. You don’t need to book. Doors open at 19:00, through the main entrance on Market Square.

## Speak at a meeting

You can ask a question or speak for 3 minutes about a subject on the agenda. Tell us by 12:00 on the Monday before the meeting. See [ask a question at a council meeting](/services/ask-a-question-at-a-council-meeting).

## Watch online

Every meeting is streamed live with subtitles. Recordings stay online for 4 years, with a written record of what was said.
