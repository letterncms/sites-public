Each of the six districts elects councillors to represent it. You can write to any councillor, but the councillors for your district are the people who know your area best.

## Contact a councillor

Every councillor has an email address made of their first name and last name: firstname.lastname@aldermoor.example. You can also leave a message for any councillor on 01632 960000, and they will call you back.

Councillors hold surgeries in their district, where you can talk to them in person. Ask the council office when the next one near you is.
