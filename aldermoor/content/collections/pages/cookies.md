Cookies are small files that a website puts on your device. This website does not use cookies to follow you, measure you or show you advertising.

## Cookies we use

We only use cookies that are strictly necessary, so you don’t have to accept them:

| Name | What it does | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- |
| None for visitors | Reading pages, searching and sending forms set no cookies | – |
| Sign-in (staff only) | Keeps council staff signed in to edit the website | When the browser closes |

## Statistics

We count how many times each page is read on our own server, without cookies and without storing your IP address, so we can improve the pages people need most.

## Videos and maps

Pages don’t load videos or maps from other websites until you choose to open them. When you follow a “Get directions” link, the map service you go to may set its own cookies.
