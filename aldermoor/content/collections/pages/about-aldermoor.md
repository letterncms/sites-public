## A city on the river

Aldermoor grew up around a crossing on the river Alder, where farmers from the moors brought their goods to market. The Market Square still holds a market every Wednesday and Saturday, as it has for more than 700 years.

Today Aldermoor is a city of 214,000 people, two universities and a busy port. It is one of the greenest cities in the region, with Moorside Park at its heart and woods and heath on every side.

## The six districts

- **Old Town**: the historic centre, Market Square and City Hall
- **Riverside**: the quays, the pool and new homes along the water
- **Northgate**: the station, the hospital and the business park
- **Heathfield**: family homes on the edge of the heath
- **Millbrook**: the old mill streets, schools and the market garden
- **Eastmoor**: the newest district, built around a lake

## Twin towns

Aldermoor has friendship links with three cities in Europe. Every summer, young people from all four cities spend a week together in one of them.
