The City of Aldermoor wants everyone to be able to use this website: to find information, apply for services and contact us, whatever device, browser or assistive technology they use. This statement explains how accessible the website is, what we know does not work yet, and how to tell us about a problem.

This statement applies to this website and to the online forms on it. It follows the model statement of the European Union for public sector websites (Directive (EU) 2016/2102).

## How accessible this website is

::: callout {"props":{"tone":"tip","text":"This website is <strong>partially compliant</strong> with the European standard EN 301 549 and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. The parts that are not compliant are listed below."}}
:::

You should be able to:

- use the whole website with a keyboard only, and always see where you are on the page
- zoom in to 400% without text being cut off or the page scrolling sideways
- change the colours, text size, line spacing and fonts in your browser
- listen to the website with a screen reader, such as NVDA, JAWS, VoiceOver or TalkBack
- control it by voice, using the names you see on links and buttons
- read the website in light or dark colours, following your device’s setting
- turn off animations, following your device’s setting for reduced motion

The text is set in a typeface designed for people with low vision, every text has a contrast of at least 4.5 to 1 with its background, and we write in plain language at a reading age of about 9 to 11.

## What is not accessible yet

### Not compliant with the accessibility rules

- **Recordings of council meetings before January 2025 have no captions.** This fails WCAG success criterion 1.2.2 (captions). We are adding captions to the recordings of the last four years and expect to finish by 31 December 2027. Every written record of these meetings is available in the minutes.
- **The search results need JavaScript.** Without it, the search page shows a message instead of results. This doesn’t fail a success criterion, but we know it makes the site harder to use for some people. Without JavaScript, you can use the [A to Z of services](/services) and the menus.

### Not covered by the accessibility rules

- **Documents published before 23 September 2018.** Some older PDF documents are not fully accessible. They are not needed for any service we offer today. If you need one in another format, ask us and we will send it within 10 working days.
- **Maps from other providers.** The “Get directions” links open a map from an outside service, which we don’t control. Every address is also written out in full on our pages.

### Disproportionate burden

We do not claim that fixing any part of this website would be a disproportionate burden.

## Preparation of this statement

This statement was prepared on 10 November 2026 and last reviewed on 10 November 2026.

It is based on an evaluation of a representative sample of 40 pages and all online forms, carried out in October 2026 by an independent accessibility specialist. They tested with automated tools, by keyboard, with screen readers on desktop and mobile, with 400% zoom and with voice control, against WCAG 2.2 level AA. We review this statement every year and after every large change to the website.

## Feedback and contact information

If something on this website doesn’t work for you, or you need information in another format, such as large print, Braille, audio, sign language or Easy Read, tell us. We will reply within 10 working days.

- Use the [contact form](/contact) and choose “Something else”
- Email info@aldermoor.example
- Call 01632 960000, Monday to Friday, 8:00 to 18:00
- Visit City Hall, Market Square 1. Staff can help you at the desk

Tell us the web address of the page, what you were trying to do, and what went wrong.

## Enforcement procedure

If you are not happy with how we respond to your feedback or your request for information in another format, you can make a [complaint](/complaints). If you are still not satisfied, you can contact the national body that monitors the accessibility of public sector websites in your country. The European Commission keeps a [list of the national monitoring bodies](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/web-accessibility).

## Technical information

This website is built with HTML, CSS and WAI-ARIA, and uses a small amount of JavaScript for search, filters and menus. Every page works without JavaScript except the search results. It is tested in current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android.

Our online payments, such as paying for a bulky waste collection, also follow the accessibility requirements of the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882).
