## What you can bring

| Free for residents | We charge for |
| --- | --- |
| Garden waste, wood, metal | Building rubble over 1 cubic metre: €15 |
| Electrical items and fridges | Tyres: €5 each |
| Paper, glass, textiles | Asbestos (pre-booked only): €25 |
| Small amounts of paint and batteries |  |

Bring proof that you live in Aldermoor, such as your council tax bill or ID card.

## Coming by car or van

Cars and small vans up to 3.5 tonnes are welcome. Trailers up to 2 metres long are fine. Larger vehicles need a business permit.

## Can’t bring it yourself?

We can collect large items from your home. [Book a bulky waste collection](/services/book-a-bulky-waste-collection).
