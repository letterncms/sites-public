## What you can do here

- Collect a passport or ID card you applied for
- Hand in documents for a building permit or a parking permit
- Get advice from the city’s money and debt team, every Friday

Visits are by appointment only. [Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) and choose Northgate as the place.

## Getting here

The service point is in the Northgate community centre, opposite the station. Buses 7 and 12 stop outside.
