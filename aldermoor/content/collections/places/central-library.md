## What you’ll find

- More than 120,000 books, films and magazines, and e-books to borrow from home
- 40 free computers, printing and scanning, and free Wi-Fi throughout
- Study rooms for 1 to 8 people, free to book
- A children’s floor with storytelling every Wednesday and Saturday

## Help with digital services

Every weekday from 10:00 to 12:00, our digital helpers can show you how to use the city’s online services, apply for things online or video-call your family. No need to book.

[Join the library](/services/join-the-library) for free if you live, work or study in Aldermoor.
