## What you can do here

- Apply for or collect a [passport or ID card](/services/apply-for-a-passport-or-id-card)
- Register a birth, a marriage or partnership, or a death
- Get advice on parking permits, council tax and building permits
- Pick up recycling bags and food waste caddies

Most services need an appointment, so you don’t have to wait. [Make an appointment](/make-an-appointment) online, or call 01632 960000.

## Getting here

City Hall is on the north side of Market Square, a five-minute walk from the central station. Trams 2 and 4 stop at Market Square. There are 12 bicycle racks by the entrance and 4 disabled parking spaces on Church Lane, 30 metres from the door.

## Help in another language

Our staff can arrange an interpreter by phone, free of charge. Tell us which language you need when you make your appointment.
