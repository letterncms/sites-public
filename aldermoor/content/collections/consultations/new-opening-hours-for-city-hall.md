In June and July 2026 we asked residents when they would like to visit City Hall. 2,118 people answered the survey, and 64 people came to our drop-in sessions.

## What you told us

- 58% of people find it hard to visit during working hours
- Thursday evening was the most popular choice for a late opening
- Many people asked for more appointments at Northgate

## What we changed

- Since 1 October 2026, City Hall is open until 20:00 every Thursday
- Northgate Service Point now opens on Friday mornings too
- You can book an appointment online up to 8 weeks ahead

Thank you to everyone who took part.
