Market Square is the heart of Aldermoor, but in summer it is one of the hottest places in the city, and it is hard to cross for people with a pushchair or a wheelchair. We have three designs for a new square, and we want to know what you think.

## The three designs

1. **Shade and seating**: 24 large trees, benches all around and a water play area.
2. **Market first**: room for 80 market stalls, with trees along the edges and a covered area for events.
3. **A green link**: a park running from City Hall to the river, with fewer stalls and a new cycle route along the side.

All three designs make the square step-free, with smooth paving, more seating and better lighting.

## What happens next

We will read every response. In spring 2027 we will publish what you told us and which design the council chooses, on this page. Work would start in 2028 at the earliest.

## Come and talk to us

Join the residents’ evening on 9 December at City Hall, or visit our stand at the winter market. You can also ask for the designs in large print or Easy Read by calling 01632 960000.
