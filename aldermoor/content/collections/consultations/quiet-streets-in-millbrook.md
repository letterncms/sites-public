Every morning, hundreds of cars cut through the streets around the two primary schools in Millbrook to avoid the traffic lights on Mill Road. Parents tell us the streets feel unsafe for children walking and cycling to school.

## What we propose

For 12 months, starting in March 2027, cars would no longer be able to drive straight through Willow Street, Brook Lane, School Street and Cedar Road. Every home would still be reachable by car, and emergency services and bin lorries could pass.

## What we want to know

- How you travel in the area today
- How the trial would affect you
- What we should measure to know whether it works
