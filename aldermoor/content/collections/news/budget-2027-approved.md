On Thursday 12 November, the city council approved the budget for 2027 by 27 votes to 12. The budget is €1.18 billion. Of this, €812 million pays for day-to-day services, €296 million for building and repairs, and €72 million for paying off loans.

## Where the money for services goes

| Area | Budget for 2027 |
| --- | --- |
| Care, health and support | €238 million |
| Schools, children and young people | €164 million |
| Streets, transport and parking | €118 million |
| Housing | €96 million |
| Waste, parks and environment | €87 million |
| Running the council | €57 million |
| Culture, sport and libraries | €52 million |

## The main choices

- We start building 350 affordable homes in Northgate and Eastmoor.
- The Housing Support Fund, which helps people who are behind with their rent, doubles to €2.4 million.
- €1.5 million goes to the design of a new Market Square, after we have heard what residents think.

To balance the budget, we save €9 million by closing one office building, moving more services online and cutting our energy use. City Hall and Northgate stay open.

## What it means for you

Property tax goes up by 2.1%, in line with rising prices. For a home worth €290,000, that is about €5 more a year. The waste charge stays the same: €312 a year if you live alone, and €398 for 2 or more people. You will get your bill at the end of February.
