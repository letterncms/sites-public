This winter we are planting 600 new trees in streets across the city. Planting starts in the week of 16 November and ends in March 2027.

## Where the trees are going

| District | New trees |
| --- | --- |
| Eastmoor | 210 |
| Northgate | 170 |
| Heathfield | 80 |
| Millbrook | 70 |
| Riverside | 45 |
| Old Town | 25 |

Most trees go to Eastmoor and Northgate because these districts have the fewest. Only 9% of Eastmoor is shaded by trees, compared with 24% in Heathfield. Residents asked for 140 of the new trees themselves.

## Why trees matter

Trees keep streets cooler in summer, soak up rainwater, clean the air and make streets nicer to walk along. We have chosen 34 different kinds of tree that can cope with hotter, drier summers. Planting many kinds also means one disease can’t harm them all.

::: quote {"props":{"text":"“A street with trees is cooler, quieter and friendlier. We are starting with the neighbourhoods that have had the fewest trees for far too long.”","cite":"Ama Mensah, deputy mayor for environment"}}
:::

## What to expect

If a tree is planted in your street, you get a letter 2 weeks before. Planting a tree takes about 2 hours. We may need to keep 1 or 2 parking spaces free on the day.

We water every new tree for its first 3 years. You can help in dry weeks by giving it a bucket of water. The 600 trees cost €1.1 million, including 3 years of care.

## Get involved

Would you like a tree in your street? [Request a tree](/services/request-a-tree). Requests made by 30 September are planted the following winter.

You can also help us plant. Join our tree-planting day in Moorside Park on Saturday 13 March 2027. Find out more on [events](/events).
