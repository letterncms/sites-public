The new playground at Weir Meadow in Millbrook opens on Saturday 7 November. Mayor Helen Achterberg will cut the ribbon at 11:00, together with the children who helped design it. Everyone is welcome.

## Designed by children

Last spring, 140 children from 3 primary schools in Millbrook drew their dream playground. Our designers turned the drawings into 3 plans, and the children voted for the winner. The most popular wishes were a big slide, water to play with and a place to hide.

## What’s there

- a wide slide built into a grassy hill, so children can slide side by side
- a roundabout set into the ground, which a wheelchair can roll straight onto
- a large nest swing that fits several children, or a child and a parent
- a sand and water area with a hand pump and channels
- a fenced area with small climbing frames for toddlers
- a quiet corner with a willow tunnel, for children who need a break from the noise

All paths are flat and wide. There are benches in the shade of new trees, and an accessible toilet in the park building.

## Opening hours and ages

The playground is open every day from 8:00 until it gets dark. It is for children up to 12. The whole playground is fenced, with 2 gates that close by themselves.

## Getting there

Weir Meadow is at the end of Mill Street, next to the river. In spring 2027 it will also be at the end of the new cycle route along the river. There are 30 bike parking spaces at the entrance.

The playground cost €420,000. If something gets broken or is unsafe, [report a problem](/report-a-problem) and we will look at it within 24 hours.
