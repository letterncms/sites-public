Work on a new cycle route along the River Alder starts on Monday 14 September. When it opens in spring 2027, people on bikes can ride from Alder Quay in Old Town to the weir in Millbrook without sharing the road with cars.

## What we are building

- a 3.8 km cycle path for both directions, 3 metres wide, kept apart from traffic by a raised kerb and a strip of plants
- a separate footpath, so people walking and cycling each have their own space
- 4 new crossings, with traffic lights that give priority to bikes at the busiest junctions
- 80 new trees, new lighting and benches along the way
- 120 bike parking spaces near Alder Quay and Riverside Pool

The route costs €6.4 million. A national grant for cycling pays €3.8 million of this.

## Why we are doing it

The number of people cycling in Aldermoor has grown by a third since 2022. But many people tell us they don’t feel safe riding next to traffic on the roads by the river, especially with children.

## During the works

We are building the route in 4 sections of about 6 weeks each, starting in Old Town. The riverside footpath stays open, with signs to show you any short diversions. Homes and businesses along the route can always be reached by car.

We write to everyone who lives next to a section 2 weeks before work there starts. If you have a question, use the [contact form](/contact) or call 01632 960000.
