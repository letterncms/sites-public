From Monday 4 January 2027, we collect food waste from every home in Aldermoor, every week, on your usual collection day.

## What you get

Between 30 November and 18 December, we deliver to every home:

- a small grey caddy with a lid, for your kitchen
- a brown food bin with a lock on the lid, to put outside on collection day
- a roll of liners for your kitchen caddy
- a leaflet with your first collection date

In Old Town, where many homes have no room for a bin, we put shared brown bins at the collection points at the end of each street.

## What goes in

All food, cooked or raw: fruit and vegetable peelings, leftovers, meat, fish and bones, bread, rice and pasta, tea bags, coffee grounds and eggshells.

Keep out packaging, liquids such as milk or oil, and anything that isn’t food.

## Why we are doing it

Food makes up about a third of what goes into grey bins in Aldermoor. When it is burned with other rubbish, it is wasted. Collected separately, we turn it into biogas and compost. The compost goes back on our parks and is given away free at Heathfield Recycling Centre.

::: quote {"props":{"text":"“Most of us throw away food without thinking. Now every peel and leftover can be turned into energy and compost. It takes a few seconds in the kitchen and makes a real difference.”","cite":"Ama Mensah, deputy mayor for environment"}}
:::

## What it costs

Nothing extra. Food waste collection is paid for from the waste charge, which stays the same in 2027.

## When you run out of liners

You can use newspaper, or buy compostable liners in most supermarkets. Don’t use plastic bags, even ones that say they break down: our plant can’t process them.

If your caddy hasn’t arrived by 18 December, use the [contact form](/contact). Find your collection day on [find your bin collection day](/services/find-your-bin-collection-day).
