Riverside Pool on Alder Quay reopened on Saturday 24 October, after 14 months of renovation. More than 2,300 people came for a swim over the opening weekend.

## What is new

- new changing rooms, with private cubicles for everyone who wants one
- a fully accessible changing room with a hoist and an adult-sized changing bench
- a learner pool with a floor that moves up and down, so it can be shallow for toddlers or deep for lessons
- hoists in both pools, and a ramp into the learner pool
- 420 solar panels on the roof, which heat about a third of the water
- better air and lighting around the pool

The pool now uses 40% less energy than before. The renovation cost €9.8 million.

## Prices stay the same

A single swim still costs €5.50 for adults and €3 for children aged 4 to 15. Children under 4 swim free, and people with an Aldermoor Pass pay half.

## Opening hours

The pool is open Monday to Friday 7:00 to 21:30, Saturday 8:00 to 18:00 and Sunday 9:00 to 16:00. Lane swimming is on weekdays 7:00 to 9:00 and 19:30 to 21:30. On Saturday morning there is a family swim with inflatables, and on Sunday at 9:00 there is a quiet swim with low lighting and no music.

## Swimming lessons

Lessons for children from 4 years old and for adults have started again. If you were on the waiting list before the renovation, you keep your place. New swimmers can join the list with the [contact form](/contact). The wait for children’s lessons is about 3 months.
