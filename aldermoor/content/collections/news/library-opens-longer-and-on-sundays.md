From Monday 2 November 2026, the Central Library on Library Lane 2 is open for 14 more hours a week.

## New opening hours

| Day | Until now | From 2 November |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday to Thursday | 10:00 to 18:00 | 10:00 to 20:00 |
| Friday | 10:00 to 18:00 | 10:00 to 18:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00 to 16:00 | 10:00 to 17:00 |
| Sunday | Closed | 12:00 to 17:00 |

## Why we are changing

In the city survey, the library came second in the list of services people value most. But many people told us they couldn’t get there before it closed. Students said they needed a quiet place to study in the evening, and parents asked for somewhere to go with children at the weekend.

The longer hours cost €310,000 a year. This pays for 6 new staff. Volunteers will help at the front desk on Sundays.

::: quote {"props":{"text":"“The library is one of the few places in the city where you are welcome without spending any money. It should be open when people can actually come, after work, after school and on Sundays.”","cite":"Mayor Helen Achterberg"}}
:::

## What’s on

On Sunday afternoons there will be family activities, such as board games, crafts and story times. Every third Saturday of the month there is a repair café, where volunteers help you fix things. You can find these on [events](/events).

The money advice drop-in stays on Wednesday from 13:00 to 16:00. Our digital helpers are there every weekday from 10:00 to 12:00, to help with computers, phones and online services.

## Join for free

Membership is free for everyone who lives, works or studies in Aldermoor. Find out how to [join the library](/services/join-the-library).
