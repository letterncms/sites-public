From Monday 5 October to Friday 4 December 2026, Station Road in Northgate is closed to traffic between the station and Mill Road.

## What we are doing

- replacing 1.2 km of water pipes that are more than 80 years old
- laying a new road surface
- making the pavements wider at the bus stops
- adding 2 new crossings near Northgate station

We work Monday to Friday from 7:00 to 19:00. We don’t start noisy work, such as breaking up the road, before 8:00. There is no work at weekends.

## Diversions and buses

- Cars and lorries follow yellow diversion signs along Canal Street.
- Buses 7 and 12 also go along Canal Street. Their stop outside the Northgate community centre moves to a temporary stop on Canal Street, about 200 metres away.
- People on bikes can push them along the pavement through the works.
- The pavement on at least one side of the road stays open all the time.

## Homes and businesses

- Northgate Service Point at Station Road 40 stays open as usual, on Tuesday and Friday 9:00 to 13:00, by appointment.
- You can reach every shop and home on foot. If you need a car at your door, for example for a disabled resident or a delivery, talk to our site team and we will make it possible.
- Your water may be turned off for a few hours while we connect the new pipes. You get a letter 2 days before.
- Put your bins out on Wednesday as usual. Our crews collect them on foot and bring them back.

## Questions or problems

Our site team is at the cabin opposite Station Road 40, Monday to Friday 7:30 to 16:00. You can also call 01632 960000 or use the [contact form](/contact). If something on the site looks unsafe, [report a problem](/report-a-problem).
