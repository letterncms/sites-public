From now until the end of March, our winter team watches the weather day and night. When the road surface is likely to freeze, our 9 gritters go out, usually in the evening or before 6:00.

## Which roads we grit first

1. **Main roads**: bus and tram routes, the roads to the hospital and fire stations, and the steep streets in Heathfield. We grit these within 3 hours, in time for the morning rush.
2. **Cycle routes and main footpaths**: including the finished parts of the new river cycle route, the main streets in Old Town, Market Square and the paths around schools.
3. **Other streets**: only when snow lies for more than a day, or more than 5 cm falls.

You can find a map of the gritting routes in [documents](/documents).

## Grit bins

There are 140 yellow grit bins across the city, on hills and at busy corners. Anyone can use the grit on roads and pavements. If a bin is empty, [report it](/report-a-problem) and we refill it within 2 working days.

## How you can help

- Clear the pavement in front of your home if you can. Do it early, before the snow is trodden down, and don’t push snow onto the road.
- Check on older or disabled neighbours, and offer to fetch shopping.
- Park so that gritters and bin lorries can get through your street.

## Bins in snow and ice

Put your bins out as usual. If we can’t reach your street safely, leave them out and we come back within 2 working days. We publish any changes in [news](/news).

## Dangerous ice

If you see dangerous ice on a main road or a fallen tree, call 01632 960000 and choose option 1. This line takes urgent calls 24 hours a day.
