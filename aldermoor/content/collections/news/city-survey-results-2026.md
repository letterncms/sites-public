Every 2 years we ask residents what they think of Aldermoor and of our services. This year 6,400 people from all 6 districts took part, online and on paper. Thank you to everyone who did.

## What you told us

- 81% like living in Aldermoor, up from 78% in 2024.
- 64% feel safe in their street after dark, up from 59%.
- The services people value most are parks and green spaces (72%), the library (58%) and bin collections (55%).
- 1 in 5 households say they found it hard to pay their bills in the past year.

## What worries you

| Concern | Residents who named it |
| --- | --- |
| The cost of housing | 67% |
| Road safety on the way to school | 41% |
| Litter and fly-tipping | 38% |
| Too few trees in their street | 26% |

People in Eastmoor and Northgate were the most likely to ask for more trees and better places for children to play.

## What we are doing about it

Your answers help us decide where the money goes. The 2027 budget, which the council will debate in November, puts more money into affordable homes and into help for people who are struggling with bills. We are also building a safe cycle route along the river, planting 600 new street trees this winter and cleaning the Old Town streets more often.
