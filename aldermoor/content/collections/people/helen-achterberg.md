Before becoming mayor, Helen worked for 15 years as a family doctor in Riverside and then led the city’s public health service. She lives in the Old Town with her partner and their two children.

## Priorities

- A city where every resident can find help quickly, online or in person
- Safe streets and well-prepared neighbourhoods
- A balanced budget that protects the services people rely on most

## Meet the mayor

The mayor holds an open hour on the first Monday of every month at City Hall, from 16:00 to 17:00. No appointment needed.
