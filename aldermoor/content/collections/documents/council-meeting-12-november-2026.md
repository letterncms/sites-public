## What’s in it

### Decisions

The council approved the budget for 2027 by 26 votes to 13, and agreed to consult residents on a greener Market Square.

### Questions from residents

Three residents asked questions about road works on Station Road, the cost of swimming lessons and lighting in Moorside Park.
