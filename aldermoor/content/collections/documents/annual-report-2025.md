## What’s in it

### Highlights

We answered 96% of requests on time, opened two new schools and planted 640 trees.

### Our finances

We spent €1.12 billion, €8 million less than planned, and kept council tax rises below inflation.

### Key figures

| Measure | 2024 | 2025 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Requests answered on time | 93% | 96% |
| Average service rating | 4.2 | 4.4 |
| Homes built | 812 | 1,034 |
