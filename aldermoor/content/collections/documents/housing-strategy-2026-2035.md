## What’s in it

### New homes

Most new homes will be built on former industrial land in Riverside and Northgate, close to public transport.

### Better homes

We will make every council home warm, dry and energy-efficient by 2035.

### Key figures

| District | New homes by 2035 |
| --- | --- |
| Riverside | 3,200 |
| Northgate | 2,600 |
| Eastmoor | 1,900 |
| Other districts | 1,300 |
