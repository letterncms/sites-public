## What’s in it

### Where the money goes

Most of the budget pays for care, schools, housing and keeping the city clean and safe. Investment in streets, schools and climate projects is €214 million.

### Where the money comes from

About half comes from national government, a quarter from council tax and fees, and the rest from rents, grants and borrowing.
