## What’s in it

### Homes

We will help 20,000 homeowners insulate their homes and switch to heat pumps, with grants and free advice.

### Transport

More room for walking, cycling and public transport, and charging points within 250 metres of every home.

### Nature

600 new street trees a year and 40 hectares of new green space, which also keep the city cooler in summer.
