## What’s in it

### Information

Every new web page and document meets WCAG 2.2 level AA, and important letters are available in Easy Read.

### Buildings and streets

All public buildings will have a step-free entrance, a hearing loop and an accessible toilet by 2028.

### Services

Staff are trained to help residents with any disability, and every service can be used by phone or in person as well as online.

### Key figures

| Goal | Deadline |
| --- | --- |
| All web forms tested with residents | 2026 |
| Hearing loops in all buildings | 2027 |
| Step-free bus stops on main routes | 2028 |
