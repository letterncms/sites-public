## Unreleased

### Added

- **Aldermoor, a city council’s website.** A complete site for a local government to start from, built for accessibility and meeting WCAG 2.2 AA in light and dark: a search-first home page, 33 services filed under 12 topics with what each costs, how long it takes and how to apply, an A to Z with search and topic filters, real search with a results page, news with press releases and works notices, events, consultations with a response form, the mayor, the executive and how the council works, public buildings with opening hours and accessibility details, downloadable documents, contact, report-a-problem and appointment forms, a feedback question on every page, an alert bar for disruption or emergencies, and an accessibility statement in the European Union’s model format. Set in Atkinson Hyperlegible Next, with photographs throughout and a library of 12 sections and two whole pages to build new pages from.
